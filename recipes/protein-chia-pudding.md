# Protein Chia Pudding

Thick, creamy vanilla chia pudding set overnight with protein powder and almond milk, topped with vanilla Greek yoghurt and fresh strawberries. A make-ahead snack or light breakfast with 19 g protein and 9 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Protein Chia Pudding" (author unknown; serves 2)

*Plan this the night before: it needs at least 6 hours (overnight) in the fridge.*

## Ingredients

### Pudding
- **250 mL (1 cup)** unsweetened vanilla almond milk
- **30 g (1 scoop)** vanilla or chocolate whey protein powder
- **48 g (4 US tbsp, about ⅓ cup)** chia seeds
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** Queen sugar-free maple-flavoured syrup
- **¼ tsp** vanilla essence

### To serve
- **60 g (¼ cup)** vanilla Greek yoghurt (e.g. Chobani Fit vanilla)
- **75 g (½ cup)** strawberries, sliced

## Method
1. Pour the almond milk into a large measuring jug, add the protein powder and whisk until smooth.
2. Add the chia seeds, maple syrup and vanilla essence and whisk well, breaking up any lumps.
3. Leave for 5 min, then whisk again to break up any chia seeds that have clumped together.
4. Pour evenly between 2 jars, glasses or mugs.
5. Cover and refrigerate overnight (at least 6 hours), until thick and set.
6. Top each with half the yoghurt and strawberries before serving.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** 1 jar (about 230 g)
- **Fridge:** 4 days in sealed jars (add strawberries on the day) · **Freezer:** not suitable
- **Reheat:** not needed, serve cold
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** double the batch for 4 days of snacks. Add the yoghurt and strawberries at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Kept at 2 serves.** The original already meets the snack targets, so no ingredients changed.
- **Added vanilla essence (¼ tsp) to the ingredients list**: it was in the method but not listed.
- **Chia seeds written in grams (48 g)**, since US and Australian tablespoons differ.
- **Queens SF maple syrup topping written as Queen sugar-free maple-flavoured syrup**, the product name in Australian supermarkets.

## Notes & swaps
- **Chocolate:** use chocolate protein powder and add 1 tsp cocoa powder. No change to protein.
- **Dairy-free:** use a pea or soy protein powder and coconut or soy yoghurt. Protein drops about 2 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use frozen strawberries, thawed. No change to protein.
- **More protein:** use 100 g plain Greek yoghurt per serve as the topping. Adds about 7 g protein and 35 kcal.
