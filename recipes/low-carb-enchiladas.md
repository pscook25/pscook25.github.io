# Low Carb Enchiladas

Low-carb wraps rolled around spiced chicken, spinach and black beans, baked under a smoky cumin-tomato sauce and melted cheese until golden, with a fresh tomato and cucumber salad on the side. Comfort food with 57 g protein and 21 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Low Carb Enchiladas" (author unknown; serves 6)

## Ingredients

### Filling
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** olive oil, plus a light spray for the dish
- **150 g (1 medium)** brown onion, sliced
- **5 g (1 tsp)** crushed garlic
- **600 g** skinless chicken breast, diced into 1 cm cubes
- **120 g (4 cups)** baby spinach, chopped
- **400 g can** black beans, drained and rinsed (about 240 g)

### Sauce
- **170 g** tomato paste
- **10 g (1 tbsp)** taco seasoning (e.g. Mingle)
- **2 tsp** ground cumin
- **1 sachet** stevia (e.g. Natvia)
- **200 mL** hot water

### Wraps & topping
- **8** Simson's Pantry mini low carb wraps (about 30 g each)
- **60 g** reduced-fat grated tasty cheese

### Side salad
- **400 g (3 medium)** tomatoes, chopped
- **400 g (2–3 Lebanese)** cucumbers, chopped
- **40 g (¼ small)** red onion, finely sliced
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** lime juice

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200°C conventional / 180°C fan-forced.
2. In a measuring jug, whisk the tomato paste, taco seasoning, cumin, stevia and hot water until smooth.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the brown onion and garlic and cook for 2 min.
4. Add the chicken and cook for 5–6 min, stirring, until browned and cooked through (internal temp 75°C).
5. Add the spinach, black beans and 250 mL (1 cup) of the sauce. Cook for 5 min, stirring, until the spinach wilts and the mixture thickens.
6. Lightly spray a large baking dish (about 30 × 20 cm) with olive oil.
7. Divide the chicken mixture down the centre of the 8 wraps and roll up firmly.
8. Place the enchiladas seam-side down in the dish.
9. Spread the remaining sauce lightly and evenly over the top (too much makes them soggy), then sprinkle with the cheese.
10. Bake for 15–20 min, until the cheese is melted and the edges are golden and crisp.
11. Meanwhile, toss the tomato, cucumber, red onion and lime juice in a bowl.
12. Serve 2 enchiladas per person with the salad on the side.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** 2 enchiladas (about 330 g) plus 210 g salad
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 2 months (enchiladas only, wrapped well)
- **Reheat:** microwave covered for 2–2½ min until piping hot (75°C), or reheat in a 180°C oven for 15 min (from frozen, thaw overnight first) for crisper edges
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** bake the enchiladas ahead and portion. Make the salad fresh, or chop the veg ahead and add the lime juice at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 6 serves to 4.** Each serve is 2 enchiladas, matching the card's nutrition panel (8 wraps instead of 16).
- **Chicken increased from 500 g for 6 serves to 600 g for 4 serves (150 g per serve)**: lifts protein to 57 g.
- **Black beans increased from ½ US cup to a full 400 g can**: adds fibre and protein.
- **Cheese reduced from 1 US cup for 6 serves to 60 g reduced-fat for 4 serves**: keeps sat fat at 4 g and sodium in range.
- **Tomato paste and taco seasoning scaled down** (170 g and 1 tbsp) to suit 4 serves and keep sodium under 700 mg.
- **Veggie stock cube removed**: a single cube adds over 200 mg sodium per serve, and the taco seasoning and cumin carry the flavour.
- **Ground cumin reduced from 1 tbsp to 2 tsp** and **hot water from 250 mL to 200 mL**, to match the smaller batch of sauce.
- **Added the tomato and cucumber side salad** (suggested on the card) with quantities.
- **Oven temp given as conventional and fan-forced.**

## Notes & swaps
- **Wraps:** Simson's Pantry mini wraps are at Woolworths and Coles. Any low-carb or high-fibre mini wrap works. Check the label for sodium, which varies a lot.
- **Vegetarian (from the card):** replace the chicken with 600 g diced pumpkin (roasted at 200°C for 20 min) plus an extra 400 g can of black beans. Protein drops to roughly 25 g per serve, below target. Add 300 g crumbled firm tofu to the filling to reach about 33 g.
- **Budget:** use chicken thigh fillets, trimmed. Protein drops about 4 g and sat fat rises about 1 g per serve.
- **Sautéed greens side:** swap the salad for 400 g green beans or broccolini, steamed. Fibre and energy stay about the same.
