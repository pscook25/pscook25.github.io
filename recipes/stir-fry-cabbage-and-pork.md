# Stir-Fry Cabbage & Pork

Lean pork strips stir-fried with buttery, tender-crisp cabbage, celery and onion, finished with soy, garlic and ginger and served over brown rice. A cheap, filling stir-fry with 43 g protein and 12 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Stir-Fry Cabbage & Pork" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Pork
- **600 g** pork fillet (tenderloin) or lean pork stir-fry strips, trimmed and thinly sliced
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** canola or peanut oil

### Vegetables
- **20 g (1 tbsp)** Kerrygold butter (or any salted butter)
- **240 g (about 3 stalks)** celery, sliced on the diagonal
- **1 kg (about ½ medium)** green cabbage, finely shredded
- **320 g (2 medium)** brown onions, sliced
- **3 cloves (15 g)** garlic, finely chopped
- **10 g (2 tsp)** fresh ginger, finely grated

### Sauce
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** salt-reduced soy sauce
- **½ tsp** ground white pepper

### To serve
- **600 g** cooked brown rice (from 200 g / 1 cup uncooked, or microwave brown rice pouches)
- **2** spring onions, thinly sliced

## Method
1. Cook the brown rice according to the packet, about 25–30 min. (Microwave pouches can be heated at step 13 instead.)
2. Heat half the oil in a large wok or deep frying pan over high heat until shimmering.
3. Add half the pork in a single layer and stir-fry for 2–3 min, until browned and just cooked through (internal temp 70°C, no pink juices).
4. Transfer to a plate. Repeat with the remaining oil and pork.
5. Reduce heat to medium-high and add the butter to the wok. Heat until bubbly and foaming.
6. Add the celery and stir-fry for 1 min.
7. Add the onion, garlic and ginger and stir-fry for 1 min until fragrant.
8. Add the cabbage in two batches, tossing until each batch wilts slightly.
9. Stir-fry for 2–3 min more, until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
10. Return the pork and any resting juices to the wok and toss to combine.
11. Add the soy sauce and white pepper and toss well.
12. Cook for 1 min more until everything is hot and glossy, then remove from the heat.
13. Heat the cooked rice (or microwave pouches) until steaming.
14. Divide the rice among 4 bowls, top with the stir-fry and scatter with spring onion.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 600 g
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** not suitable (the cabbage goes watery and limp). Rice can be frozen separately for up to 1 month.
- **Reheat:** microwave covered for 2–2½ min, stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C). Or toss in a hot wok for 2 min.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the rice ahead, and the whole stir-fry if eating within 3 days. Add the spring onion at serving. For the crunchiest result, slice the veg ahead and stir-fry on the day (about 10 min).

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric (20 mL tbsp).
- **Butter cut from 2 US tbsp (about 28 g) per serve to 5 g per serve, plus 5 mL oil per serve**: the original butter alone was about 14 g sat fat per serve. This keeps a buttery flavour with about 5 g sat fat per serve.
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos (4 tbsp per serve) replaced with salt-reduced soy sauce (10 mL per serve), and "salt to taste" removed**: the original amount would be well over 2,000 mg sodium per serve. Sodium is now about 555 mg.
- **Pre-cooked shredded pork replaced with 150 g raw pork fillet per serve, stir-fried**: lean and easy to find, with 43 g protein per serve. A cooking step was added.
- **Cabbage increased from 2 US cups to 250 g per serve**: lifts fibre and volume.
- **Celery and onion given in grams** (60 g and 80 g per serve).
- **Added garlic, ginger and white pepper** to make up for the flavour lost when the salt and aminos were cut.
- **Added brown rice (150 g cooked per serve)**: the original was about 425 kcal. This lifts energy into the 500–650 kcal range and adds fibre.
- **Added spring onion** to serve.

## Notes & swaps
- **Leftover roast pork:** use 480 g shredded cooked lean pork (fat removed) instead of the raw fillet and skip steps 2–4. Add it at step 10. Protein is similar. If the pork was salted or glazed, cut the soy to 30 mL.
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos:** if you prefer it, use 30 mL (1½ tbsp) in total instead of the soy. Sodium stays about the same.
- **Gluten-free:** use salt-reduced tamari. No change to protein.
- **Dairy-free:** replace the butter with another 1 tbsp oil. Sat fat drops to about 2 g per serve.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the pork with 600 g firm tofu (pressed, cubed and pan-fried until golden) plus 200 g frozen shelled edamame, added at step 10. Protein drops to roughly 32 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use 5-star lean pork mince instead of fillet. Protein drops about 3 g and sat fat rises about 1 g per serve, still within targets.
- **Lower carb:** halve the rice to 300 g and add 200 g frozen shelled edamame at step 10. Energy stays about 550 kcal, and protein rises to about 48 g.
