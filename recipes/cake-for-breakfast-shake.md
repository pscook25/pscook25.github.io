# Cake for Breakfast Shake

A thick, frosty vanilla shake blended with oats and a pinch of salt so it tastes like cake batter, finished with a few sprinkles. A fun breakfast or snack with 28 g protein.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Shake Recipes: Cake for Breakfast" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Shake
- **40 g (½ cup)** rolled oats
- **30 g (1 scoop)** vanilla whey protein powder
- **5 mL (1 tsp)** vanilla extract
- **1 sachet** stevia (e.g. Natvia)
- **Pinch (about 0.25 g)** salt
- **250 mL (1 cup)** cold water
- **1 handful (about 6 cubes)** ice

### Topping
- **4 g (1 tsp)** hundreds and thousands (sprinkles)

## Method
1. Blitz the oats in a blender for 20–30 sec until finely ground.
2. Add the water, protein powder, vanilla, stevia, salt and ice.
3. Blend for 45–60 sec until thick and smooth.
4. Pour into a glass and scatter the sprinkles on top.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 400 mL
- **Fridge:** 1 day in a sealed bottle (it thickens as the oats soak, so shake or add water) · **Freezer:** not suitable once blended
- **Reheat:** not needed
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** grind a jar of oats ahead (40 g per shake) to save a step. Blend fresh.

## Changes from original
- **Oats reduced from ⅔ US cup (about 60 g) to 40 g**: brings energy from about 375 kcal into the snack range (295 kcal). Protein is still 28 g.
- **Salt and sprinkles given quantities**, since the card said "dash of".
- **Quantities written in grams and Australian metric.**

## Notes & swaps
- **As a full breakfast:** use the card's 60 g oats and 250 mL skim milk instead of water. About 460 kcal and 39 g protein.
- **Dairy-free:** use a vanilla pea or soy protein powder. Protein drops about 1 g.
- **Gluten-free:** use certified gluten-free oats or quinoa flakes.
- **Birthday-cake flavour:** add ¼ tsp butter-vanilla essence. No change to nutrition.
