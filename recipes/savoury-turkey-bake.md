# Savoury Turkey Bake

Golden egg-and-oat muffins packed with garlicky turkey mince, onion, broccoli and cauliflower, finished with a dash of hot sauce. A high-protein, fibre-rich breakfast you can bake once and reheat all week.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Savoury Turkey Bake" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Turkey
- **10 g (2 tsp)** olive oil
- **150 g (1 medium)** brown onion, finely chopped
- **500 g** turkey mince

### Egg mixture
- **400 g (about 12)** egg whites, or liquid egg whites from a carton
- **200 g (4)** eggs
- **200 g (2¼ cups)** rolled oats
- **300 g** broccoli, finely chopped
- **300 g** cauliflower, finely chopped
- **4 g (2 tsp)** garlic powder
- **1.5 g (¼ tsp)** salt
- **¼ tsp** cracked black pepper

### To cook & serve
- **8 g** olive oil spray, for greasing (about 2 tsp oil)
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** hot sauce (e.g. sriracha), to serve

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 180°C conventional / 160°C fan-forced. Grease a 12-hole muffin tin (⅓ cup holes) with the olive oil spray.
2. Heat the 2 tsp olive oil in a large non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat.
3. Add the onion and cook for 3 min, until softened.
4. Add the turkey mince and cook for 6–8 min, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned and no longer pink. Set aside to cool for 5 min.
5. In a large bowl, whisk the egg whites and eggs together until combined.
6. Stir in the oats, broccoli, cauliflower, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
7. Add the cooled turkey mixture and stir well.
8. Divide the mixture evenly among the muffin holes (they'll be full).
9. Bake for 18–20 min, until puffed, golden and set in the centre (a skewer comes out clean, internal temp 72°C).
10. Rest in the tin for 5 min, then run a knife around the edges and lift out.
11. Serve 3 per person with hot sauce drizzled over the top.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** 3 muffins (about 330 g)
- **Fridge:** 4 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 2 months (wrap individually or freeze on a tray first, then bag)
- **Reheat:** microwave 3 muffins for 60–90 sec (from frozen 2½–3 min, turning halfway), until piping hot (75°C)
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** bake the whole batch ahead. Add the hot sauce at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Ground turkey is called turkey mince here.
- **Oven temp converted from 350°F to 180°C conventional / 160°C fan-forced.**
- **Single-serve dish changed to a 12-hole muffin tin** (the card offered muffin tins as an option), and bake time increased from 8–10 min to 18–20 min to cook the larger batch and the raw veg through.
- **Turkey changed from 85 g cooked to 125 g raw per serve, with a step to cook it**: makes the turkey quantity clear and lifts protein.
- **Added 1 whole egg per serve** alongside the 3 egg whites: helps the muffins hold together and adds flavour.
- **Oats changed from ½ US cup (about 40 g) to 50 g per serve**: adds fibre.
- **Veg changed from 1 US cup to 150 g per serve**, split evenly between broccoli and cauliflower.
- **Added onion and olive oil** to cook the turkey and add flavour.
- **Garlic salt (no quantity) replaced with garlic powder plus ¼ tsp salt in total**: keeps sodium under 700 mg once the hot sauce is added.
- **Hot sauce quantity specified (5 mL per serve)**, since the card left the seasoning open.

## Notes & swaps
- **Vegetarian:** replace the turkey with 400 g canned brown lentils (no added salt, drained) and 100 g finely chopped mushrooms. Protein drops to roughly 37 g per serve.
- **Gluten-free:** use certified gluten-free oats or quinoa flakes. No change to protein.
- **Budget:** use 5-star lean beef mince or chicken mince instead of turkey. Protein stays about the same. Beef adds about 1 g sat fat per serve.
- **Turkey mince availability:** Coles and Woolworths both stock it. If you can't find it, chicken mince is the closest swap.
- **Frozen veg:** a 600 g bag of frozen broccoli and cauliflower works. Thaw and squeeze dry first so the muffins aren't soggy.
