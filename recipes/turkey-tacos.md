# Turkey Tacos

Spiced turkey mince and kidney beans simmered with fresh pico de gallo and wild rice, piled into crisp cos lettuce shells and finished with your favourite hot sauce. Light, crunchy tacos with 44 g protein and very little salt.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Turkey Tacos" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Turkey
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** olive oil
- **600 g** lean turkey mince
- **6 g (1 tbsp)** mild chilli powder (no added salt)
- **10 g (1 tbsp)** onion powder
- **6 g (2 tsp)** garlic powder
- **½ tsp** smoked paprika
- **¼ tsp** cayenne pepper
- **400 g can** no-added-salt red kidney beans, drained and rinsed (about 320 g)

### Pico de gallo
- **320 g (2 large)** tomatoes, finely diced
- **80 g (½ medium)** brown onion, finely diced
- **20 g (½ bunch)** coriander, leaves chopped
- **30 g (1)** jalapeño or long green chilli, seeded and finely chopped
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** lime juice

### To serve
- **480 g** cooked brown and wild rice blend (2 × 250 g microwave pouches, e.g. SunRice)
- **320 g (12–16 large leaves)** cos lettuce leaves
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** hot sauce of choice

## Method
1. Make the pico de gallo: combine the tomato, brown onion, coriander, chilli and lime juice in a bowl.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
3. Add the turkey mince, chilli powder, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika and cayenne.
4. Sauté for 6–8 min, breaking up the mince, until browned and fully cooked (no pink, internal temp 74°C).
5. Add the kidney beans and pico de gallo and sauté for 2–3 min, until hot and well mixed.
6. Meanwhile, heat the rice according to the packet.
7. Remove the pan from the heat and stir through the rice.
8. Divide the mixture evenly among the lettuce shells (3–4 per person) and top with hot sauce.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 3–4 lettuce tacos (about 460 g filling plus 80 g lettuce)
- **Fridge:** 4 days in airtight containers (filling only) · **Freezer:** 3 months (filling only)
- **Reheat:** microwave the filling covered for 2–2½ min (from frozen, thaw overnight first), stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C)
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the full filling ahead and portion it. Wash and dry the lettuce leaves ahead and store in a container with paper towel. Add the hot sauce at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Ground turkey is called turkey mince and romaine is called cos here.
- **Turkey reduced from 170 g to 150 g per serve**: protein is still 44 g with the beans.
- **Chilli powder reduced from 1¼ tsp per serve to 1 tbsp in total, and specified as mild**: Australian chilli powder is much hotter than a US chili powder blend.
- **Onion powder and garlic powder adjusted to 1 tbsp and 2 tsp in total**, and cayenne to ¼ tsp in total.
- **Added smoked paprika** for a smoky, mild flavour in place of some of the chilli.
- **Wild rice changed to a brown and wild rice blend (120 g cooked per serve)**: pure wild rice is hard to find at Coles or Woolworths. The card's ½ cup is read as cooked.
- **Pico de gallo given a recipe and quantities**, since the card used a ready-made ½ cup.
- **Added kidney beans (80 g per serve) and olive oil**: lifts fibre from about 5 g to 13 g and energy into the 500–650 kcal range.
- **Hot sauce given a quantity (5 mL per serve).**

## Notes & swaps
- **Turkey mince availability:** Coles and Woolworths both stock it. Chicken mince is the closest swap, with the same protein.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the turkey with 400 g firm tofu, crumbled, plus a second can of kidney beans. Protein drops to roughly 30 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use 5-star lean beef mince. Protein stays about the same, and sat fat rises about 1 g per serve.
- **Taco shells:** swap the lettuce for 8 small corn tortillas (2 per person) and halve the rice. Energy and fibre stay about the same.
