# Minestrone Soup

A thick, chunky Italian vegetable soup loaded with two kinds of beans, tender chicken and small pasta, finished with baby spinach and a little parmesan. The beans and chicken push the protein and fibre well past target, and it reheats beautifully for the week.

**Source:** Original

## Ingredients
### Soup base
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** olive oil, divided
- **400 g** chicken breast, trimmed and cut into 2 cm cubes
- **150 g (1 medium)** brown onion, diced
- **200 g (2 medium)** carrots, diced 1 cm
- **120 g (2 stalks)** celery, diced 1 cm
- **3 cloves** garlic, crushed
- **40 g (2 tbsp)** tomato paste
- **2 tsp** dried Italian herbs
- **1** bay leaf
- **250 g (2 medium)** potatoes, unpeeled, scrubbed and diced 1.5 cm
- **500 mL (2 cups)** salt-reduced chicken stock
- **1 L (4 cups)** water
- **400 g** can diced tomatoes, no added salt

### Vegetables and beans
- **200 g (1 medium)** zucchini, diced 1.5 cm
- **150 g** green beans, trimmed and cut into 3 cm lengths
- **400 g** can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed (about 240 g)
- **400 g** can borlotti beans, drained and rinsed (about 240 g)
- **60 g (about 2 cups, loosely packed)** baby spinach

### Pasta and to serve
- **100 g** small dried pasta (ditalini or small macaroni)
- **30 g** parmesan, finely grated
- **1 tbsp (20 mL)** lemon juice
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Method
1. Heat 10 mL olive oil in a large heavy-based saucepan or stockpot over medium-high heat.
2. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook, turning occasionally, until golden on the outside, 4-5 min. It does not need to be cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
3. Reduce the heat to medium and add the remaining 10 mL oil to the same pan.
4. Add the onion, carrot and celery and cook, stirring, until softened, 6-7 min.
5. Add the garlic, tomato paste, Italian herbs and bay leaf and stir for 1 min until fragrant.
6. Add the potato, stock, water and canned tomatoes. Bring to the boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
7. Simmer, partly covered, until the potato is just tender, 12-15 min.
8. Add the zucchini, green beans, cannellini beans, borlotti beans and the browned chicken with any juices.
9. Simmer until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 75°C) and the vegetables are tender, 10-12 min.
10. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in a separate saucepan of unsalted boiling water until al dente, 8-9 min, then drain.
11. Remove the bay leaf from the soup. Stir through the spinach until wilted, about 1 min.
12. Stir in the lemon juice and season with black pepper. Taste before adding any salt, as the parmesan and stock already contribute sodium.
13. Divide the pasta between 4 bowls, ladle over the soup and top each with about 7 g (1 heaped tbsp) grated parmesan.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** approx. 550 g per serve (about 120 g cooked pasta plus 430 g soup)
- **Fridge:** 4 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 3 months (freeze the soup without the pasta)
- **Reheat:** microwave covered 3-4 min on high, stirring halfway, until steaming hot (at least 75°C throughout). Or heat in a saucepan over medium heat for 6-8 min, adding a splash of water if too thick.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** Make the soup up to step 9 and store. Keep the cooked pasta in a separate container so it doesn't go soggy, and add the spinach, lemon juice and parmesan when reheating or serving.

## Changes from original
- No source recipe was supplied, so this is an original minestrone built around the nutrition targets.
- Added 400 g chicken breast and two kinds of beans to lift protein above 30 g per serve (the traditional version is usually under 15 g).
- Used both cannellini and borlotti beans, plus potato, carrot and unpeeled vegetables, to get fibre well above 8 g.
- Used salt-reduced stock diluted with water, no-added-salt canned tomatoes, rinsed canned beans and unsalted pasta water to keep sodium under 700 mg.
- Limited the oil to 20 mL and the parmesan to 30 g to keep saturated fat low and energy within 500-650 kcal.
- Used Australian ingredient names (zucchini, tomato paste) and measures.

## Notes & swaps
- **Vegetarian:** Leave out the chicken, use vegetable stock and add a third can of beans or 200 g firm tofu. Protein drops to roughly 30 g per serve.
- **Dairy-free:** Skip the parmesan and add 1 tbsp nutritional yeast per serve. Protein drops by about 3 g and sodium falls by about 120 mg.
- **Gluten-free:** Use gluten-free small pasta or 100 g cooked brown rice. Protein is similar.
- **Budget:** Use chicken thigh (trim the fat, which adds about 1 g saturated fat per serve) or swap the borlotti for a second can of cannellini. Protein is roughly unchanged.
- **Higher sodium tolerance:** Use regular stock for more flavour, but expect sodium to rise by 250-400 mg per serve and push it over target.
- **Lower carb:** Halve the pasta to 50 g and add another 100 g zucchini. This saves about 85 kcal and 18 g carbs per serve and drops protein by about 2 g.
