# Chicken Veggie Stir-Fry

Juicy strips of chicken and crunchy snow peas, broccoli and capsicum, wok-tossed in soy with garlic, onion and a pinch of chilli, folded through brown rice. A quick, clean stir-fry with 49 g protein and 12 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Chicken Veggie Stir-Fry" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- **600 g** skinless chicken breast, cut into thin strips
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** olive or canola oil

### Vegetables
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** olive or canola oil
- **480 g (2 heads)** broccoli, cut into small florets
- **400 g** snow peas or sugar snap peas, trimmed
- **320 g (1 large)** red capsicum, sliced

### Sauce & seasoning
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** salt-reduced soy sauce
- **12 g (4 tsp)** garlic powder
- **10 g (1 tbsp)** onion powder
- **¼ tsp** dried chilli flakes, or to taste

### To serve
- **600 g** cooked brown rice (from 200 g / 1 cup uncooked, or microwave pouches)

## Method
1. Cook or heat the brown rice according to the packet. Keep warm.
2. Heat 2 tsp oil in a large wok or frying pan over high heat.
3. Stir-fry the chicken in 2 batches for 3–4 min each, until golden and cooked through (internal temp 75°C). Transfer to a plate.
4. Reduce heat to medium-high and add the remaining 2 tsp oil.
5. Add the broccoli, snow peas and capsicum with the garlic powder, onion powder and chilli flakes.
6. Add 20 mL of the soy sauce and stir-fry for 3–4 min, until the vegetables are bright and tender-crisp.
7. Return the chicken to the wok and add the rice.
8. Add the remaining soy sauce and toss for 1–2 min, until everything is coated and hot.
9. Remove from the heat and divide among 4 bowls.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 530 g
- **Fridge:** 4 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 2 months (the veg will soften a little)
- **Reheat:** microwave covered for 2½–3 min (from frozen, 5–6 min), stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C)
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the rice and chop the veg ahead. The whole stir-fry keeps well for meal prep. For extra heat, add hot sauce at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Red pepper flakes are called chilli flakes here.
- **Cooked chicken (170 g per serve) changed to 150 g raw per serve, stir-fried**: protein is still 49 g.
- **Brown rice increased from ½ US cup cooked to 150 g cooked per serve.**
- **Snap peas increased from ½ US cup to 100 g per serve, and broccoli from ½ US cup to 120 g per serve.**
- **Added red capsicum (80 g per serve)**: adds colour, crunch and fibre. Together these bring the dish to 545 kcal and 12 g fibre.
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos (no quantity) replaced with 40 mL salt-reduced soy sauce in total**: keeps sodium at about 465 mg and is easy to find.
- **Garlic powder and onion powder reduced from 1 tsp each per serve to 4 tsp and 3 tsp in total.**
- **Added oil** for stir-frying, since the card didn't say how to stop sticking.

## Notes & swaps
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos:** use 30 mL in place of the soy sauce if you prefer it. Sodium stays about the same.
- **Shortcut:** use 480 g shredded BBQ chicken breast meat (skin removed) and skip step 3. Sodium rises about 250 mg per serve, so cut the soy to 30 mL.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the chicken with 600 g firm tofu, cubed and pan-fried until golden. Protein drops to roughly 32 g per serve.
- **Gluten-free:** use salt-reduced tamari. No change to protein.
- **Budget:** use a 1 kg bag of frozen stir-fry vegetables instead of fresh. Fibre drops about 2 g per serve.
