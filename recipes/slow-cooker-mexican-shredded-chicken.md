# Slow Cooker Mexican Shredded Chicken

Tender chicken thighs slow-cooked with cumin, smoked paprika and chilli in a rich tomato sauce, then shredded and tossed with black beans, corn and capsicum. Bulk-cook it and freeze it in portions for burrito bowls, tacos or lettuce cups; it already packs 58 g protein and 15 g fibre per serve before you add anything.

## Ingredients

### Spice mix
- **2 tsp** ground cumin
- **2 tsp** smoked paprika
- **1 tsp** ground coriander
- **1 tsp** dried oregano
- **1 tsp** mild chilli powder (use 2 tsp for more heat)
- **¼ tsp** salt

### Slow cooker
- **800 g** skinless chicken thigh fillets, fat trimmed
- **150 g (1 medium)** brown onion, diced
- **4 cloves (16 g)** garlic, crushed
- **300 g (2 medium)** red capsicums, deseeded and sliced into strips
- **400 g can** diced tomatoes, no added salt
- **40 g (2 tbsp)** tomato paste, no added salt

### Stir through
- **2 × 400 g cans** black beans, drained and rinsed (about 480 g drained)
- **300 g (2 cups)** frozen corn kernels
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** lime juice (about 2 limes)

### To serve
- **240 g (about 4 cups)** shredded iceberg lettuce
- **160 g (⅔ cup)** reduced-fat plain Greek yoghurt
- **15 g (½ bunch)** coriander, leaves picked
- **1** lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Mix the cumin, smoked paprika, ground coriander, oregano, chilli powder and salt in a small bowl.
2. Rub half the spice mix all over the chicken thighs.
3. Spread the onion, garlic and capsicum over the base of a 5–6 L slow cooker.
4. Lay the chicken on top of the vegetables.
5. Stir the diced tomatoes, tomato paste and remaining spice mix together, then pour over the chicken.
6. Cover and cook on LOW for 6 hours (or HIGH for 3½ hours), until the chicken pulls apart easily and reaches an internal temp of at least 75°C.
7. Lift the chicken onto a board and shred it with two forks.
8. Return the chicken to the slow cooker and stir in the black beans and frozen corn.
9. Cook uncovered on HIGH for 30 min, stirring once, until the corn is hot and the sauce has thickened.
10. Stir in the lime juice and taste; add extra chilli if you like, rather than salt.
11. To serve, spoon the chicken over the lettuce and top with the yoghurt, coriander leaves and lime wedges.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 500 g shredded chicken mix per serve (lettuce, yoghurt and coriander extra)
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 3 months
- **Cool quickly:** spread the mix into shallow containers and refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking. For the freezer, portion into zip-lock bags, press flat, label and date.
- **Reheat:** thaw overnight in the fridge, then microwave covered for 3–4 min, stirring halfway, until steaming hot (75°C). From frozen, use the defrost setting for 6–8 min first, or heat flat bags gently in a covered saucepan with a splash of water for 10–12 min.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** freeze the chicken, beans and sauce together. Add the lettuce, yoghurt, coriander and lime fresh at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Built from your idea as a complete freezable meal base at 4 serves**, converted to Australian metric.
- **Chicken thigh rather than breast**: it stays moist over 6 hours and shreds better; trimmed thigh keeps sat fat at 3 g per serve.
- **Black beans and corn added**: chicken alone is about 250 kcal and has no fibre. These lift it to 535 kcal and 15 g fibre per serve, and they freeze well.
- **Own spice mix instead of a taco seasoning sachet**: sachets add about 400–500 mg sodium per serve. Salt is limited to ¼ tsp across the batch, and no-added-salt tomatoes and paste are used, keeping sodium at 560 mg.
- **No stock and no oil**: the tomatoes and chicken juices make enough liquid, and leaving out oil keeps fat down.
- **Reduced-fat Greek yoghurt in place of sour cream**: adds protein and cuts sat fat.

## Notes & swaps
- **Bulk batches:** double everything in a 6–7 L slow cooker (cook time stays the same; allow 45 min in step 9). Don't fill the cooker more than two-thirds full.
- **Using it in other meals:** this base is already 535 kcal per serve, so to stay under 650 kcal add no more than about 110 kcal, e.g. 1 small wholemeal tortilla (about 110 kcal), or ½ cup cooked brown rice (about 110 kcal) in place of half the lettuce. For bigger bases, use a smaller portion of chicken mix (it has protein to spare).
- **Breast swap:** use 800 g chicken breast. Energy drops about 40 kcal and protein stays similar, but it can dry out; cook on LOW for 4–5 hours only.
- **Chipotle:** add 1–2 chipotle chillies in adobo (La Morena, in the Mexican aisle at Coles and Woolworths) with the tomatoes for a smoky heat. Adds about 60 mg sodium per serve.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the chicken with 2 extra cans of black beans plus 400 g firm tofu, crumbled and added with the beans in step 8. Protein drops to about 30 g per serve.
- **Dairy-free:** swap the yoghurt for 30 g mashed avocado per serve. Protein drops about 4 g; fat rises about 4 g (mostly unsaturated).
- **Budget:** use 1 can of black beans and 1 can of red kidney beans (no added salt), and frozen sliced capsicum. Nutrition stays about the same.
