# Sweet Egg Scramble

A soft vanilla-and-cinnamon egg scramble swirled with jammy mixed berries, piled onto wholegrain toast and sprinkled with toasted almonds. Like French toast in a pan, with 43 g protein and 12 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Sweet Egg Scramble" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Scramble
- **400 g (about 12)** egg whites, or liquid egg whites from a carton
- **200 g (4)** eggs
- **60 g (2 scoops)** vanilla whey protein powder
- **4 sachets** stevia (e.g. Natvia)
- **5 mL (1 tsp)** vanilla extract
- **400 g** frozen mixed berries
- **8 g (2 tsp)** olive oil, or olive oil spray

### To serve
- **8 slices (about 336 g)** wholegrain high-protein bread (e.g. Burgen Soy-Lin)
- **60 g** flaked almonds, toasted
- **1 tsp** ground cinnamon

## Method
1. In a large bowl, whisk the egg whites, eggs, protein powder, stevia and vanilla until smooth and no lumps of powder remain.
2. Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan over medium heat.
3. Pour in the egg mixture and leave for 1 min, until it starts to set around the edges.
4. Gently push the egg from the edges into the centre with a spatula. Keep going for 2–3 min, until about half cooked.
5. Add the frozen berries and keep scrambling for 3–4 min, until the eggs are cooked through with no runny parts and the berries are warmed and bursting.
6. Meanwhile, toast the bread.
7. Put 2 slices of toast on each plate and pile the scramble on top.
8. Scatter with the toasted almonds and dust with cinnamon.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 340 g scramble plus 2 slices toast
- **Fridge:** 2 days in airtight containers (scramble only) · **Freezer:** not suitable (the egg goes rubbery and watery)
- **Reheat:** microwave the scramble for 60–90 sec, stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C)
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** best cooked fresh (it takes 15 min). To prep ahead, whisk the egg mixture the night before and keep it chilled in a jug. Toast the bread and add almonds and cinnamon at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric.
- **Protein powder written as 15 g per serve instead of ½ scoop**, since scoop sizes vary between brands.
- **Added 1 whole egg per serve** alongside the 3 egg whites: makes the scramble softer and creamier.
- **Mixed berries changed from ⅔ US cup to 100 g per serve**, and specified as mixed berries (the card just said frozen berries).
- **"Choice of a veggie on the side" replaced with 2 slices of wholegrain high-protein toast and toasted almonds**: the original was about 165 kcal with little fibre, and no vegetable was named. Toast and almonds bring it to 550 kcal and 12 g fibre and suit the sweet flavour better.
- **Vanilla increased from ¼ tsp to 1 tsp in total** (rounded up slightly when scaling).
- **Added a little olive oil** for cooking, since the card didn't say how to stop the eggs sticking.

## Notes & swaps
- **Bread:** Burgen Soy-Lin is a good match. Any wholegrain or wholemeal sourdough works. Check sodium on the label, as some loaves push this past 700 mg.
- **Dairy-free:** use a vanilla pea or soy protein powder. Protein drops about 1–2 g per serve.
- **Gluten-free:** use a gluten-free wholegrain bread (e.g. Venerdi). Protein drops about 6 g and fibre about 3 g per serve, still on target.
- **Nut-free:** replace the almonds with 15 g pepitas per serve. Protein rises about 1 g.
- **Budget:** skip the protein powder and use 2 whole eggs per serve instead of 1. Protein drops to roughly 37 g per serve and sat fat rises about 1 g.
