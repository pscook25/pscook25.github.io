# Granola Breakfast Bowl

Crunchy maple-cinnamon granola with toasted almonds, cashews, walnuts and seeds, chewy apricots and sultanas, piled over thick Greek yoghurt with fresh strawberries and raspberries. The yoghurt brings the protein and the nuts, seeds and berries bring 12 g fibre. The batch makes enough granola for about 6 more breakfasts.

**Source:** Gordon Ramsay's granola recipe (YouTube video), from your own notes on it

## Ingredients

### Granola (makes about 715 g, 10 serves of 70 g)
- **270 g (3 cups)** rolled oats (not quick oats)
- **50 g** almonds, roughly chopped
- **45 g** cashews, roughly chopped
- **45 g** walnuts, roughly chopped
- **30 g (3 tbsp)** pepitas (pumpkin seeds)
- **25 g (2 tbsp)** sunflower seeds
- **15 g (1 tbsp)** chia seeds
- **80 mL (⅓ cup)** maple syrup
- **80 mL (⅓ cup)** olive oil (light or extra virgin)
- **5 mL (1 tsp)** ground cinnamon
- **5 mL (1 tsp)** vanilla extract
- **1 g (pinch)** salt

### Add after baking
- **40 g** dried apricots, chopped
- **35 g (¼ cup)** sultanas

### To serve (4 bowls)
- **1 kg** fat-free plain Greek yoghurt (e.g. Chobani 0%)
- **300 g** strawberries, hulled and sliced
- **300 g** raspberries (fresh, or frozen and thawed)

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 160°C conventional / 140°C fan-forced. Line a large baking tray (or two smaller ones) with baking paper.
2. In a large bowl, mix the oats, almonds, cashews, walnuts, pepitas, sunflower seeds and chia seeds.
3. In a small jug, whisk the maple syrup, olive oil, cinnamon, vanilla and salt.
4. Pour the wet mixture over the dry ingredients and stir for 1–2 min, until everything is evenly coated.
5. Spread the mixture over the tray in an even layer about 1 cm deep, pressing it down lightly with a spatula for bigger clusters.
6. Bake for 15 min.
7. Remove the tray, gently turn the granola with a spatula, then return to the oven.
8. Bake for 10–15 min more, until deep golden and fragrant. It will still feel slightly soft but crisps as it cools.
9. Cool completely on the tray, about 30 min. Don't stir while cooling, so the clusters set.
10. Break into clusters and stir through the apricots and sultanas.
11. For each bowl, spoon 250 g Greek yoghurt into a bowl.
12. Top with 70 g (about ⅔ cup) granola, 75 g strawberries and 75 g raspberries.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 470 g per bowl (70 g granola, 250 g yoghurt, 150 g berries)
- **Fridge:** 3 days for yoghurt and berries in containers · **Freezer:** granola 3 months in a sealed bag. Granola keeps 3 weeks in an airtight jar in the pantry.
- **Reheat:** not needed, serve cold. Frozen granola can be eaten straight from the freezer or thawed for 10 min.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** bake the granola ahead (this batch covers 10 serves). Portion yoghurt and berries into containers for up to 3 days. Add the granola at serving so it stays crunchy.

- **Oil: olive oil chosen over coconut oil** (the source offered either): coconut oil would add about 6 g sat fat per 70 g of granola.
- **Maple syrup: the lower amount (⅓ cup) chosen** from the ⅓–½ cup range, to keep sugars down.
- **Mixed nuts set at 140 g (about 1 cup) split between almonds, cashews and walnuts.**
- **Seeds set at 70 g in total** (pepitas, sunflower and chia), since the source gave no quantity.
- **Dried fruit set at 75 g in total** (apricots and sultanas), since the source gave no quantity.

- **Added an oven temp, bake time and stirring step**: the source didn't give a method, so this uses a standard low-and-slow granola bake.

## Notes & swaps
- **Kids' version:** swap the maple syrup for honey (same amount), as the source suggests. Add 1 tbsp freeze-dried strawberries per bowl at serving. No change to protein.
- **Treat version:** stir 40 g dark chocolate chips through the cooled granola. Adds about 20 kcal and 1 g sat fat per serve.
- **Coconut version:** add 20 g (¼ cup) desiccated coconut with the oats. Adds about 1 g sat fat per serve, still well within target.
- **Gluten-free:** use certified gluten-free oats, or replace with quinoa flakes. No change to protein.
- **Nut-free:** replace the nuts with 140 g extra pepitas and sunflower seeds. Protein rises about 1 g per serve.
- **Dairy-free:** use a high-protein soy yoghurt. Protein drops to roughly 25 g per serve. Add 15 g vanilla pea protein to the yoghurt to stay above 30 g.
- **Budget:** use the cheapest mixed nuts bag (raw, unsalted) instead of three types, and frozen mixed berries instead of fresh. No real change to protein.
