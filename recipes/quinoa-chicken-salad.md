# Quinoa Chicken Salad

Tender poached chicken tossed with fluffy quinoa, chickpeas, kale, red onion and toasted pepitas in a bright lemon, cumin and vinegar dressing. A hearty, meal-prep-friendly lunch with 58 g protein and 13 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Quinoa Chicken Salad" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Salad
- **700 g** skinless chicken breast fillets
- **160 g (¾ cup)** quinoa, rinsed (makes about 480 g cooked)
- **320 mL** water, for the quinoa
- **400 g can** no-added-salt chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- **160 g (about 5 cups)** kale, stems removed, leaves finely shredded
- **120 g (1 small)** red onion, finely chopped
- **40 g (¼ cup)** pepitas (pumpkin seeds), toasted

### Dressing
- **2** lemons, zested and juiced (about 80 mL juice)
- **60 mL (3 tbsp)** white wine vinegar
- **30 mL (1½ tbsp)** extra virgin olive oil
- **4 g (2 tsp)** ground cumin
- **1.5 g (¼ tsp)** salt
- **½ tsp** cracked black pepper

## Method
1. Put the quinoa and water in a saucepan and bring to the boil.
2. Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 12–15 min until the water is absorbed. Rest, covered, for 5 min, then fluff with a fork and spread out to cool.
3. Meanwhile, put the chicken in a saucepan, cover with cold water and bring to a gentle simmer.
4. Reduce heat to low, cover and poach for 12–15 min, until cooked through (internal temp 75°C).
5. Lift out the chicken, rest for 5 min, then shred or slice.
6. Toast the pepitas in a dry frying pan over medium heat for 2–3 min, until they start to pop. Tip onto a plate.
7. In a jar, shake the lemon zest, lemon juice, vinegar, oil, cumin, salt and pepper until combined.
8. Put the kale in a large bowl, add 2 tbsp of the dressing and massage with your hands for 1 min, until softened.
9. Add the chicken, quinoa, chickpeas and red onion to the kale.
10. Pour over the remaining dressing and toss well. Scatter with the pepitas.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 470 g
- **Fridge:** 4 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** not suitable
- **Reheat:** not needed, serve cold or at room temperature
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** make the whole salad ahead (kale holds up well once dressed). Keep the pepitas separate and add at serving so they stay crunchy.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Garbanzo beans are called chickpeas here.
- **Chicken changed from 170 g cooked to about 130 g cooked (175 g raw) per serve, with a poaching step**: protein is still 58 g with the quinoa, chickpeas and pepitas.
- **Quinoa increased from ½ US cup cooked (about 90 g) to 120 g cooked per serve**, with cooking steps.
- **Chickpeas set at 100 g per serve** and specified as no added salt.
- **Kale changed from 1¼ US cups to 40 g per serve, and massaged** so it's tender.
- **Lemon reduced from 1 per serve to 2 in total**, and white wine vinegar from 2 US tbsp per serve to 60 mL in total: one lemon plus 2 tbsp vinegar per serve would be very sharp.
- **Ground cumin reduced from 2 US tbsp per serve to 2 tsp in total**: the card's amount (about 8 tbsp for 4 serves) would overpower the salad.
- **Added olive oil to the dressing and toasted pepitas**: the original was about 280 kcal. These bring it to 615 kcal and help the dressing coat the salad.
- **Salt set at ¼ tsp in total** (the card said "to taste").

## Notes & swaps
- **Shortcut:** use 520 g shredded BBQ chicken breast meat (skin removed) and skip poaching. Sodium rises about 250 mg per serve, so leave out the salt.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the chicken with a second can of chickpeas and 300 g firm tofu, cubed and pan-fried. Protein drops to roughly 30 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use brown rice instead of quinoa. Protein drops about 1 g per serve.
- **Baby spinach:** use 160 g baby spinach instead of kale and skip the massaging step.
