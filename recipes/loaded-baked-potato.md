# Loaded Baked Potato

Fluffy baked potatoes split open and piled with smoky spiced kidney beans, cottage cheese and tasty cheese grilled until melty, cool Greek yoghurt, fresh pico de gallo and spring onion. A hearty, cheap lunch with 44 g protein and 16 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Loaded Baked Potato" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Potatoes
- **1.2 kg (4 medium, about 300 g each)** washed potatoes (e.g. Sebago or Royal Blue), skin on

### Beans
- **5 mL (1 tsp)** olive oil
- **400 g can** no-added-salt red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- **1 tsp** smoked paprika
- **1 tsp** ground cumin
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** water

### Toppings
- **500 g** light cottage cheese (e.g. Bulla Light)
- **80 g** reduced-fat grated tasty cheese
- **200 g** fat-free plain Greek yoghurt (e.g. Chobani 0%)
- **80 g (4)** spring onions, sliced

### Pico de gallo
- **320 g (2 large)** tomatoes, finely diced
- **80 g (½ medium)** brown onion, finely diced
- **20 g (½ bunch)** coriander, leaves chopped
- **30 g (1)** jalapeño or long green chilli, seeded and finely chopped
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** lime juice

## Method
1. Prick each potato 5–6 times with a fork.
2. Microwave 2 potatoes at a time on high for 8–10 min, turning halfway, until a skewer slides easily into the centre. Repeat with the remaining 2.
3. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add the kidney beans, paprika, cumin and water and cook for 3–4 min, stirring and lightly mashing, until hot and thick.
4. Make the pico de gallo: combine the tomato, brown onion, coriander, chilli and lime juice in a bowl.
5. Preheat the oven grill to high.
6. Put the potatoes on a baking tray, cut a deep cross in the top of each and squeeze to open.
7. Spoon the beans into the potatoes, then top with the cottage cheese and sprinkle with the grated cheese.
8. Grill for 1–2 min, until the cheese has melted and is starting to brown.
9. Top each potato with 50 g Greek yoghurt, the pico de gallo and spring onion.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 1 potato with toppings (about 690 g)
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers (potato and beans; store pico and yoghurt separately) · **Freezer:** not suitable (the potato goes grainy)
- **Reheat:** microwave the potato and beans covered for 2–3 min until piping hot (75°C), then add the cottage cheese and grill or microwave 30 sec more
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the potatoes and beans ahead. Make the pico up to 2 days ahead. Add the cottage cheese, grated cheese, yoghurt and spring onion at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Green onion is called spring onion, and the broiler is the grill.
- **Potato increased from 1 small (about 150 g) to 1 medium (300 g) per serve**: lifts energy and fibre.
- **Cottage cheese reduced from 170 g to 125 g per serve, specified as light**: keeps sodium under 700 mg.
- **Pico de gallo given a recipe and quantities**, since the card used a ready-made ½ cup. The card's "salsa" topping is the same pico.
- **Spring onion reduced from ½ US cup per serve to 4 spring onions in total.**
- **Added smoky kidney beans, Greek yoghurt and 20 g reduced-fat cheese per serve**: the original was about 265 kcal. These bring it to 560 kcal, 44 g protein and 16 g fibre.

## Notes & swaps
- **Oven-baked:** bake the potatoes at 200°C conventional / 180°C fan-forced for 50–60 min, until crisp-skinned and tender.
- **Sweet potato:** use 4 × 300 g sweet potatoes, microwaved for 8–10 min per pair. Fibre rises slightly. No real change to protein.
- **Vegetarian:** already vegetarian.
- **Budget:** skip the grated cheese. Protein drops about 6 g per serve, still above 30 g, and sat fat drops to about 2 g.
- **Dairy-free:** replace the cottage cheese and yoghurt with 300 g crumbled firm tofu, seasoned with lime and cumin, and a dollop of mashed avocado. Protein drops to roughly 30 g per serve.
