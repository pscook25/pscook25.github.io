# Loaded Bagels Three Ways

A toasted wholemeal bagel loaded with all three classic toppings: smashed avo and cottage cheese with cherry tomatoes and basil, smoked salmon on cottage cheese with cucumber and dill, and a swipe of hummus. Served with a Greek yoghurt and raspberry cup for 42 g protein and 15 g fibre. It goes over the sodium target (see below).

**Source:** Recipe card, "Loaded Low Carb Bagels" (author unknown; serves 1)

**Sodium target missed (990 mg vs 700 mg).** The bagel, cottage cheese and smoked salmon are all naturally salty, and the salmon alone adds about 255 mg per serve. Cutting them enough to reach 700 mg would lose what makes this dish. See Notes & swaps for a lower-sodium version (about 625 mg).

## Ingredients

### Bagels
- **4 (about 90 g each)** wholemeal bagels, halved

### Smashed avo topping
- **120 g (1 small)** avocado, flesh only
- **120 g** light cottage cheese (e.g. Bulla Light)
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** lemon juice
- **240 g (about 16)** cherry tomatoes, sliced
- **Small handful** fresh basil leaves, torn

### Salmon topping
- **120 g** light cottage cheese
- **60 g** smoked salmon
- **160 g (1 Lebanese)** cucumber, thinly sliced
- **2 tsp** fresh dill, chopped

### Hummus layer
- **80 g (4 tbsp)** light hummus

### Yoghurt cup
- **700 g** fat-free plain Greek yoghurt (e.g. Chobani 0%)
- **400 g** frozen raspberries, thawed

### To season
- **½ tsp** cracked black pepper

## Method
1. In a bowl, mash the avocado, 120 g cottage cheese and lemon juice with a fork until combined but still a little chunky.
2. Toast the bagel halves until golden.
3. Spread 1 tbsp hummus over the cut side of each of 4 bagel halves (the bottoms).
4. Spread the smashed avo mixture over the hummus, then top with the cherry tomatoes and basil.
5. Spread the remaining 120 g cottage cheese over the other 4 bagel halves (the tops).
6. Top with the smoked salmon, cucumber slices and dill.
7. Season all halves with pepper.
8. Divide the Greek yoghurt among 4 bowls and top with the raspberries.
9. Serve each person 1 avo-hummus half, 1 salmon half and a yoghurt cup.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** 1 bagel (2 loaded halves, about 290 g) plus 275 g yoghurt and raspberries
- **Fridge:** 2 days for toppings in separate airtight containers · **Freezer:** not suitable (bagels can be frozen plain for 3 months)
- **Reheat:** not needed. Toast the bagels fresh.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** mix the smashed avo (press plastic wrap onto the surface), slice the cucumber and tomatoes, and portion the yoghurt and raspberries the night before. Toast and assemble at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric.
- **Abe's Low Carb Bagels replaced with wholemeal bagels**: Abe's isn't sold in Australia. Wholemeal bagels (Coles, Woolworths and bakeries) give more fibre than white.
- **Renamed "Loaded Bagels Three Ways"** since wholemeal bagels aren't low carb.
- **All three toppings combined into one serve**: avo and hummus share one half, salmon and cottage cheese go on the other.
- **Cottage cheese reduced from ¼ US cup per topping to 30 g per half (60 g per serve)**, and salmon from 50 g to 15 g per serve: keeps sodium as low as possible without losing the toppings.
- **Avocado reduced from ¼ avocado to about 30 g per serve**, and hummus from 2 tbsp to 20 g (1 tbsp) per serve.
- **Lime or lemon juice set as lemon (10 mL in total).**
- **Salt removed**: the bagel, cottage cheese and salmon are already salty. Pepper is kept.
- **Added a Greek yoghurt and raspberry cup (175 g and 100 g per serve)**: each original bagel was about 270–330 kcal with 15–26 g protein. The cup brings the meal to 555 kcal and 42 g protein.

## Notes & swaps
- **Lower-sodium version (about 625 mg per serve):** leave out the smoked salmon and use light ricotta instead of cottage cheese. Protein drops to about 36 g per serve, and sat fat rises to about 6 g. Leaving out the salmon alone brings sodium to about 735 mg.
- **Low-carb bread:** use 2 slices of a lower-carb high-protein loaf (e.g. Herman Brot or Burgen Soy-Lin) per serve instead of a bagel. Carbs drop about 20 g, protein rises 5–10 g, and sodium is similar.
- **Vegetarian:** skip the salmon and add a second smashed avo half. Protein drops about 3 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use canned pink salmon (drained) instead of smoked salmon. Sodium drops about 150 mg per serve and protein stays similar.
- **Pre-workout snack (from the card):** half a serve (one bagel half) works well on its own.
