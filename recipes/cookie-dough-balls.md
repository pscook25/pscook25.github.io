# Cookie Dough Balls

Soft, no-bake balls that taste like raw cookie dough: peanut butter, oats and vanilla protein with little pockets of no-sugar choc chips. Two balls give you 16 g protein for a grab-and-go snack.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Cookie Dough Balls" (author unknown; makes 16 balls)

## Ingredients

### Dough
- **90 g (1 cup)** rolled oats
- **120 g (about ⅓ cup + 2 tbsp)** natural peanut butter, smooth or crunchy (no added salt)
- **100 g (3⅓ scoops)** vanilla whey protein powder
- **40 g (⅓ cup)** linseed (flaxseed) meal
- **60 mL (¼ cup)** Queen sugar-free maple-flavoured syrup
- **80 mL (⅓ cup)** unsweetened almond milk, plus extra if needed
- **1.25 mL (¼ tsp)** vanilla essence

### Mix-in
- **30 g (about 2½ tbsp)** Noshu no-added-sugar dark choc chips

## Method
1. Put the oats, peanut butter, protein powder, linseed meal, maple syrup, almond milk and vanilla in a large bowl.
2. Mix with a spatula, then your hands, until it forms a firm dough, like cookie dough.
3. If it's crumbly, add more almond milk 1 tsp at a time until it holds together.
4. Fold in the choc chips.
5. Roll into 16 even balls (about 30 g each).
6. Place on a lined tray or plate and refrigerate for 1 hour until firm.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** 2 balls (about 60 g)
- **Fridge:** 7 days in an airtight container · **Freezer:** 3 months (eat straight from frozen or thaw 10 min)
- **Reheat:** not needed
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** make the full batch on the weekend and portion 2 per snack box.

## Changes from original
- **Serve defined as 2 balls (8 serves per batch of 16).**
- **Protein powder increased from 1 scoop to 100 g (about 3⅓ scoops)**: lifts protein from about 8 g to 16 g per serve.
- **Added linseed meal (40 g)**: lifts fibre to the 4 g snack target.
- **Peanut butter reduced slightly from ½ US cup (about 130 g) to 120 g, and choc chips from ¼ US cup (about 45 g) to 30 g**: keeps sat fat at 3 g per serve.
- **Almond milk increased from 2 US tbsp to 80 mL**: the extra protein powder and linseed meal need more liquid.
- **Optional stevia left out**: the maple syrup and protein powder are sweet enough. Add ½ tsp if you like it sweeter.

## Notes & swaps
- **Choc chips:** Noshu is at Coles and Woolworths. Any dark choc chips work, but regular ones add about 3 g sugar per serve.
- **Vegan / dairy-free:** use a vanilla pea or soy protein powder. Protein drops about 1 g per serve, and you may need a little more almond milk.
- **Nut-free:** use a seed butter (e.g. sunflower or tahini) instead of peanut butter. Protein drops about 2 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use linseed meal or LSA, whichever is cheaper. No real change to protein.
- **Pre-workout:** 2 balls 60–90 min before training.
