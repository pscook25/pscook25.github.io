# PB Sandwich Shake

A thick peanut butter and "jam" shake: strawberry or chocolate protein blended with natural peanut butter and frozen raspberries. It tastes like a PB&J sandwich, with 29 g protein and 8 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Shake Recipes: PB Sandwich Shake" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Nutrition (per serve, estimated)
| Energy | Protein | Carbs | Sugars | Fat | Sat fat | Fibre | Sodium |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

*Estimates based on AUSNUT 2011–13 / FSANZ data and typical labels (whey protein powder, 30 g per scoop). Snack targets apply: 200–300 kcal, ≥15 g protein, ≥4 g fibre, sat fat ≤3 g, sodium ≤350 mg.*

## Ingredients

### Shake
- **30 g (1 scoop)** strawberry or chocolate whey protein powder
- **20 g (1 Aus tbsp)** natural smooth peanut butter (no added salt)
- **100 g** frozen raspberries
- **250 mL (1 cup)** cold water
- **1 handful (about 6 cubes)** ice

## Method
1. Put the water, peanut butter and raspberries in a blender.
2. Add the protein powder and ice.
3. Blend for 45–60 sec until thick and smooth.
4. Pour into a glass and serve straight away.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 450 mL
- **Fridge:** 1 day in a sealed bottle (shake before drinking) · **Freezer:** not suitable once blended
- **Reheat:** not needed
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** portion the raspberries into freezer bags. Blend fresh.

## Changes from original
- **Protein powder reduced from 1½ scoops to 1 scoop (30 g)**: protein is still 29 g with the peanut butter and raspberries.
- **Peanut butter reduced from 2 US tbsp (about 32 g) to 20 g, and specified as natural**: brings sat fat from about 4.5 g to 3 g and energy into the snack range.
- **Added frozen raspberries (100 g)**: they're the "jam" in the sandwich. They lift fibre from about 3 g to 8 g and make the shake thicker.

## Notes & swaps
- **Chocolate PB:** use chocolate protein and swap the raspberries for 100 g frozen banana. Fibre drops to about 4 g, and sugars rise about 10 g.
- **Dairy-free:** use a pea or soy protein powder. Protein drops about 1 g.
- **Nut-free:** use sunflower seed butter. Protein drops about 1 g.
- **Bigger version (from the card):** use 1½ scoops and 2 tbsp peanut butter. About 400 kcal, 37 g protein and 4.5 g sat fat (above the snack targets).
