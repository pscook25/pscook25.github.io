# Energy Muffins

Soft, moist wholemeal muffins with banana, grated apple and cinnamon, boosted with vanilla protein and Greek yoghurt. Two muffins give you 19 g protein and slow-release carbs before a workout.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Energy Muffins" (author unknown; makes 7–8 muffins, serves 2)

## Nutrition (per serve, estimated)
| Energy | Protein | Carbs | Sugars | Fat | Sat fat | Fibre | Sodium |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 225 kcal / 950 kJ | 19 g | 25 g | 12 g | 4 g | 1 g | 5 g | 175 mg |

*A serve is 2 muffins. Estimates based on AUSNUT 2011–13 / FSANZ data and typical labels (whey protein powder, 30 g per scoop). Snack targets apply: 200–300 kcal, ≥15 g protein, ≥4 g fibre, sat fat ≤3 g, sodium ≤350 mg.*

## Ingredients

### Wet
- **130 g (1 large)** ripe banana, peeled
- **150 g (1 medium)** red or green apple, peeled and grated
- **50 g (1)** egg
- **100 g** fat-free plain Greek yoghurt (e.g. Chobani 0%)
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** unsweetened almond milk

### Dry
- **75 g (½ cup)** wholemeal plain flour
- **60 g (2 scoops)** vanilla whey protein powder
- **15 g (1 tbsp)** chia seeds
- **4 g (1 tsp)** baking powder
- **1 tsp** ground cinnamon

### For the tin
- **2 g** olive oil spray

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200°C conventional / 180°C fan-forced. Spray 8 holes of a muffin tin (⅓ cup holes) with olive oil spray.
2. Mash the banana in a large bowl until smooth.
3. Add the apple, egg, yoghurt and almond milk and stir to combine.
4. Add the flour, protein powder, chia seeds, baking powder and cinnamon and mix until just combined. Don't overmix.
5. Spoon the mixture evenly into the 8 muffin holes.
6. Bake for 15 min, until risen and springy when pressed (a skewer comes out clean).
7. Cool in the tin for 5 min, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** 2 muffins (about 140 g)
- **Fridge:** 4 days in an airtight container · **Freezer:** 3 months (wrap individually)
- **Reheat:** eat cold, or microwave 2 muffins for 20–30 sec (from frozen, 45–60 sec) until warm
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** bake a batch on the weekend and freeze in pairs for grab-and-go snacks.

## Changes from original
- **Serves changed from 2 to 4 (2 muffins each, 8 muffins per batch)**: a serve of 4 muffins would be above the snack energy range.
- **Plain flour changed to wholemeal plain flour, and chia seeds added**: lifts fibre from about 2 g to 5 g per serve.
- **Protein powder (optional on the card) increased to 2 scoops, and Greek yoghurt added**: lifts protein from about 4 g to 19 g per serve.
- **Almond milk increased from 1 US tbsp to 40 mL**: the extra protein powder needs more liquid (the card's protein note also adds extra milk).
- **Oven temp given as conventional and fan-forced.**

## Notes & swaps
- **Dairy-free:** use a pea or soy protein powder and a plain soy yoghurt. Protein drops about 2 g per serve.
- **Gluten-free:** use a gluten-free plain flour blend. Fibre drops about 1 g per serve.
- **Budget:** skip the protein powder and use 200 g Greek yoghurt instead of 100 g. Protein drops to roughly 9 g per serve (below the snack target), but they're still a good fibre-rich snack.
- **Pear:** swap the apple for a grated pear. No change to protein.
