# Healthy Fried Rice

Nutty brown rice wok-tossed with tender chicken, fluffy scrambled egg, a heap of mixed veg, garlic and spring onion, seasoned with soy and a little chilli. A takeaway-style favourite with 57 g protein, ready in 25 minutes.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Healthy Fried Rice" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Main
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** olive or canola oil, divided
- **400 g (about 12)** egg whites, or liquid egg whites from a carton
- **200 g (4)** eggs
- **500 g** skinless chicken breast, diced into 1.5 cm pieces
- **2 cloves (10 g)** garlic, finely chopped
- **700 g** frozen mixed vegetables (peas, corn, carrot, beans)
- **480 g** cooked brown rice, chilled (from 160 g / ¾ cup uncooked, or 2 × 250 g microwave pouches)

### Seasoning
- **10 g (1 tbsp)** onion powder
- **4 g (2 tsp)** chilli powder
- **30 mL (1½ tbsp)** salt-reduced soy sauce
- **60 g (4)** spring onions, sliced

## Method
1. In a jug, whisk the egg whites and eggs together.
2. Heat 1 tsp of the oil in a large non-stick wok or frying pan over medium heat.
3. Pour in the egg mixture and scramble continuously for 3–4 min, until just set. Transfer to a plate.
4. Increase heat to high and add the remaining oil.
5. Add the chicken and stir-fry for 4–5 min, until golden and cooked through (internal temp 75°C).
6. Add the garlic and stir-fry for 30 sec until fragrant.
7. Add the frozen vegetables and stir-fry for 3 min until hot.
8. Add the rice, onion powder and chilli powder and stir-fry for 2–3 min, breaking up any clumps, until the rice is hot.
9. Return the egg to the wok, add the soy sauce and toss for 1 min until everything is coated.
10. Remove from the heat and stir through most of the spring onion. Serve topped with the rest.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 540 g
- **Fridge:** 4 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 2 months
- **Reheat:** microwave covered for 2½–3 min (from frozen, 5–6 min), stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C). Cool and refrigerate leftover rice within 1 hour of cooking.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the rice a day ahead (chilled rice fries better). The whole dish can be cooked ahead and portioned. Add the spring onion garnish at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric.
- **Cooked chicken (85 g per serve) changed to 125 g raw diced chicken per serve, with a cooking step**: lifts protein to 57 g.
- **Added 1 whole egg per serve** alongside the 3 egg whites: softer, richer scrambled egg.
- **Brown rice increased from ½ US cup cooked to 120 g cooked per serve.**
- **Mixed veg increased from 1 US cup to 175 g per serve**: lifts fibre to 10 g.
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos (no quantity) replaced with 30 mL salt-reduced soy sauce in total**: keeps sodium under 700 mg and is easy to find.
- **Chilli powder reduced from 1 tsp per serve to 2 tsp in total**: Australian chilli powder is much hotter than a US chili powder blend.
- **Onion powder reduced from 1 tsp per serve to 1 tbsp (3 tsp) in total.**
- **Added oil, garlic and spring onion** for cooking and flavour.

## Notes & swaps
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos:** use 25 mL in place of the soy sauce if you prefer it. Sodium stays about the same.
- **Shortcut:** use 380 g shredded BBQ chicken breast meat (skin removed) and add it at step 8. Sodium rises about 200 mg per serve, so cut the soy to 20 mL.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the chicken with 400 g firm tofu, cubed and pan-fried, plus 150 g frozen edamame. Protein drops to roughly 38 g per serve.
- **Gluten-free:** use salt-reduced tamari. No change to protein.
- **Budget:** use prawns or leftover roast pork instead of chicken. Protein stays about the same.
