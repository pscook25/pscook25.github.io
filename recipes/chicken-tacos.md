# Chicken Tacos

Crisp cos lettuce cups filled with warm spiced refried beans, smoky paprika chicken and a quick fresh pico de gallo, with avocado and brown rice on the side. A light, crunchy taco night with 46 g protein and 12 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Chicken Tacos" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- **600 g** skinless chicken breast fillets
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** olive oil
- **1 tsp** smoked paprika
- **1 tsp** ground cumin

### Pico de gallo
- **320 g (2 large)** tomatoes, finely diced
- **80 g (½ medium)** brown onion, finely diced
- **20 g (½ bunch)** coriander, leaves chopped
- **30 g (1)** jalapeño or long green chilli, seeded and finely chopped
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** lime juice

### Beans
- **400 g can** refried beans (e.g. Old El Paso)
- **60 g (4)** spring onions, finely sliced
- **¼ tsp** dried chilli flakes

### To serve
- **320 g (about 16 large leaves)** cos lettuce leaves
- **120 g (1 small)** avocado, sliced
- **400 g** cooked brown rice (2 × 250 g microwave pouches, or from 135 g / ⅔ cup uncooked)

## Method
1. Mix the paprika and cumin and rub over the chicken.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
3. Cook the chicken for 5–6 min per side, until golden and cooked through (internal temp 75°C). Rest for 5 min.
4. Meanwhile, make the pico de gallo: combine the tomato, brown onion, coriander, chilli and lime juice in a bowl.
5. Heat the refried beans in a small saucepan over low-medium heat for 3–4 min, stirring, until warm.
6. Stir the spring onion, chilli flakes and half the pico de gallo into the beans.
7. Heat the brown rice according to the packet.
8. Slice the chicken into thin strips.
9. Spoon the bean mixture into the lettuce leaves (about 4 per person) and top with the chicken and remaining pico de gallo.
10. Serve with the avocado and brown rice on the side.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 4 lettuce tacos plus 100 g rice and 30 g avocado (about 540 g in total)
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers (chicken, beans and rice; pico 2 days) · **Freezer:** 2 months for chicken and bean mixture only
- **Reheat:** microwave chicken and beans covered for 1½–2 min, stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C)
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the chicken, bean mixture and rice ahead. Make the pico on the day if you can. Wash the lettuce ahead, but slice the avocado and assemble at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Romaine is called cos, green onion is called spring onion, and red pepper flakes are chilli flakes.
- **Cooked chicken (170 g per serve) changed to 150 g raw per serve, with a spiced cooking step**: protein is still 46 g with the beans.
- **Refried beans changed from ½ US cup black refried beans to 100 g per serve of standard refried beans**: black refried beans are hard to find in Australia.
- **Pico de gallo given a recipe and quantities**, since the card used a ready-made ½ cup.
- **Chilli flakes reduced from ¼ tsp per serve to ¼ tsp in total.**
- **Added avocado and brown rice on the side**: the original was under the energy and fibre targets for 4 serves. These bring it to 535 kcal and 12 g fibre.
- **Removed "salt to your liking"**: the refried beans already carry most of the sodium.

## Notes & swaps
- **Black beans:** for a black-bean version, roughly mash a 400 g can of black beans (drained) with 2 tbsp water and ½ tsp cumin. Sodium drops about 150 mg per serve.
- **Shortcut:** use 480 g sliced BBQ chicken breast meat (skin removed). Sodium rises about 250 mg per serve.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the chicken with 400 g firm tofu, crumbled and pan-fried with the spices, plus a 400 g can of black beans. Protein drops to roughly 30 g per serve.
- **Corn tortillas:** swap the lettuce for 8 small corn tortillas (2 per person) and skip the rice. Energy and fibre stay about the same.
