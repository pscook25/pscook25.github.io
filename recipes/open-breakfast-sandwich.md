# Open Breakfast Sandwich

Crisp wholegrain high-protein toast spread with creamy smashed avocado, topped with soft scrambled eggs, balsamic-wilted spinach and fresh tomato. A café-style breakfast with 36 g protein and 10 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Open Breakfast Sandwich" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Toast & avocado
- **8 slices (about 336 g)** wholegrain high-protein bread (e.g. Burgen Soy-Lin)
- **160 g (1 large)** avocado, flesh mashed

### Eggs & spinach
- **8 g (2 tsp)** olive oil, or olive oil spray
- **120 g (4 cups loosely packed)** baby spinach
- **25 mL (1¼ tbsp)** balsamic vinegar
- **500 g (about 15)** egg whites, or liquid egg whites from a carton
- **300 g (6)** eggs

### To serve
- **400 g (4 small)** tomatoes, sliced
- **½ tsp** cracked black pepper

## Method
1. In a bowl, whisk the egg whites and eggs together.
2. Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan over low-medium heat.
3. Add the spinach and balsamic and toss for 1–2 min, until the spinach wilts and shrinks.
4. Pour in the egg mixture and leave for 1 min until the edges start to set.
5. Gently stir and fold for 3–4 min more, until the eggs are cooked through and firm with no runny parts.
6. Meanwhile, toast the bread to your liking.
7. Spread the mashed avocado over the 8 slices of toast.
8. Put 2 slices of toast on each plate and divide the egg and spinach over the top.
9. Top with the tomato slices and season with pepper.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** 2 open sandwiches (about 470 g)
- **Fridge:** 2 days (egg and spinach only, in airtight containers) · **Freezer:** not suitable
- **Reheat:** microwave the egg and spinach for 60–90 sec, stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C). Then build the sandwich on fresh toast.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** best made fresh. To prep ahead, cook the egg and spinach and chill it. Toast the bread, mash the avocado and slice the tomato at serving so nothing goes soggy or brown.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric.
- **Added 1½ whole eggs per serve and increased the egg whites from 3 to about 4 per serve**: the original was about 25 g protein. This brings it to 36 g and makes the eggs softer.
- **Avocado reduced slightly from ⅓ to ¼ of a large avocado per serve (40 g)**: keeps sat fat at 5 g with the added whole eggs.
- **"Low carb, high protein bread" specified as Burgen Soy-Lin** (available at Coles and Woolworths), so the nutrition can be estimated.
- **Added balsamic vinegar to the ingredients list (about 6 mL per serve)**: it was used in the method but not listed.
- **Added a little olive oil** to cook the spinach and eggs.
- **Removed the salt**: the bread already carries most of the sodium, and salt would push it over 700 mg. Pepper is kept.
- **Spinach set at 30 g per serve** (about 1 cup loosely packed).

## Notes & swaps
- **Bread:** check sodium on the label. If your loaf is over 450 mg per 100 g, the recipe will go over 700 mg sodium per serve. Use a lower-sodium wholegrain loaf instead.
- **Gluten-free:** use a gluten-free wholegrain bread (e.g. Venerdi). Protein drops about 6 g per serve, to roughly 30 g.
- **Dairy-free:** already dairy-free.
- **Vegetarian:** already vegetarian.
- **Budget:** use 3 whole eggs per serve instead of the egg white and egg mix. Protein drops to roughly 30 g per serve, and sat fat rises about 1 g (still within target).
- **Extra protein:** add 50 g light ricotta to each serve, under the avocado. Adds about 5 g protein and 70 kcal.
