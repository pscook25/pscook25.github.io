# Lemon and Almond Roasted Salad

Roasted shredded chicken folded through a zesty lemon, parsley and Greek yoghurt dressing with golden toasted almonds, served on crisp leaves with crunchy roasted paprika chickpeas, tomato and cucumber. A fresh, creamy lunch with 57 g protein.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Lemon and Almond Roasted Salad" (author unknown; serves 2)

## Ingredients

### Roast chicken & chickpeas
- **600 g** skinless chicken breast fillets
- **2 × 400 g cans** no-added-salt chickpeas, drained, rinsed and patted dry (about 480 g)
- **1 tsp** sweet paprika
- **1 lemon**, zested (save the juice for the dressing)
- **1 tsp** cracked black pepper
- **1.5 g (¼ tsp)** salt

### Almonds
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** olive oil
- **60 g** flaked almonds

### Dressing
- **340 g (1⅓ cups)** fat-free plain Greek yoghurt (e.g. Chobani 0%)
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** lemon juice (from the zested lemon, plus extra if needed)
- **2** spring onions, finely sliced
- **10 g (½ bunch)** flat-leaf parsley, chopped

### To serve
- **240 g (2 × 120 g bags)** mixed salad leaves
- **400 g** cherry tomatoes, halved
- **320 g (2 Lebanese)** cucumbers, sliced

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 180°C conventional / 160°C fan-forced. Line a large baking tray with baking paper.
2. Mix the lemon zest, pepper and salt in a small bowl. Rub half over the chicken and put the chicken on one side of the tray.
3. Toss the chickpeas with the paprika and the remaining zest mixture and spread over the other side of the tray.
4. Roast for 20–25 min, until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 75°C) and the chickpeas are crisp. Shake the chickpeas once halfway.
5. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.
6. Add the almonds and cook, stirring constantly, for 4–5 min until toasted and golden. Tip onto a plate to cool.
7. Rest the chicken for 5 min, then shred with two forks.
8. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken, almonds, lemon juice, spring onion and parsley.
9. Fold in the yoghurt, stirring until all the chicken is coated.
10. Divide the salad leaves, tomatoes and cucumber among 4 plates or containers.
11. Top with the chicken mixture and scatter with the roasted chickpeas.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 470 g
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** not suitable (the yoghurt dressing separates)
- **Reheat:** not needed, serve cold
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** make the chicken mixture ahead and store separately from the leaves, tomato and cucumber. Keep the chickpeas in a separate container so they stay crunchy, and add at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 2 serves to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Green onion is called spring onion here.
- **Chicken set at 150 g raw per serve**: the card listed 110 g for 2 serves, which wouldn't give the protein shown on the card.
- **Almonds increased from 12 almonds (about 7 g per serve) to 15 g per serve**: more crunch and flavour.
- **Olive oil reduced from 1 US tbsp per 2 serves to 20 mL in total.**
- **Greek yoghurt specified as fat-free.**
- **Lemon pepper seasoning (1 tbsp) replaced with lemon zest and black pepper, and salt cut from ½ tsp per 2 serves to ¼ tsp in total**: most lemon pepper seasonings are high in salt.
- **Added roasted paprika chickpeas, salad leaves, tomato and cucumber**: the original had little fibre. These bring it to 12 g fibre and 540 kcal and make it a full salad.
- **Oven temp given as conventional and fan-forced.**

## Notes & swaps
- **Vegetarian:** replace the chicken with 600 g firm tofu, cubed and roasted for 25 min. Protein drops to roughly 38 g per serve.
- **Dairy-free:** use a plain high-protein soy yoghurt. Protein drops about 3 g per serve.
- **Shortcut:** use 480 g shredded BBQ chicken breast meat (skin removed) and skip roasting the chicken. Sodium rises about 250 mg per serve, so leave out the salt.
- **Budget:** use chicken thigh fillets, trimmed. Protein drops about 4 g and sat fat rises about 1 g per serve.
- **Nut-free:** replace the almonds with 60 g pepitas. Protein rises about 1 g per serve.
