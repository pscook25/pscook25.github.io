# Trail Mix

A crunchy, salty mix of roasted cashews and pepitas, light popcorn and protein-packed roasted fava beans, with a smoky paprika and cumin version or a choc version. A portable snack with 15 g protein and 9 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Trail Mix" (author unknown)

## Nutrition (per serve, estimated)
| Energy | Protein | Carbs | Sugars | Fat | Sat fat | Fibre | Sodium |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 kcal / 1250 kJ | 15 g | 17 g | 1 g | 17 g | 3 g | 9 g | 115 mg |

*A serve is about 60 g. Estimates based on AUSNUT 2011–13 / FSANZ data and typical labels (Cobs sea salt popcorn, Happy Snack Co roasted fava beans). Figures are for the savoury version. Snack targets apply: 200–300 kcal, ≥15 g protein, ≥4 g fibre, sat fat ≤3 g, sodium ≤350 mg.*

## Ingredients

### Mix
- **40 g (⅓ cup)** raw unsalted cashews
- **70 g (½ cup)** pepitas (pumpkin seeds)
- **180 g (about 1½ cups)** roasted fava beans (e.g. The Happy Snack Company)
- **20 g (about 2½ cups)** Cobs sea salt popcorn

### Savoury spice
- **½ tsp** smoked paprika
- **½ tsp** ground cumin

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200°C conventional / 180°C fan-forced. Line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Spread the cashews and pepitas on the tray.
3. Roast for 4–5 min, until lightly golden and the pepitas start to pop. Watch closely as they burn fast.
4. While still warm, sprinkle with the paprika and cumin and toss to coat.
5. Cool on the tray for 10 min.
6. Combine the cashews and pepitas with the fava beans and popcorn in a large bowl.
7. Divide into 5 containers or snack bags (about 60 g each).

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 60 g (a heaped ½ cup)
- **Fridge:** 14 days in an airtight container (or the pantry; the popcorn softens after that) · **Freezer:** not suitable
- **Reheat:** not needed
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** make the batch and portion it into 5 snack bags for the week.

## Changes from original
- **Serve defined as about 60 g (5 serves per batch).**
- **Added roasted fava beans (180 g)**: lifts protein from about 5 g to 15 g per serve and fibre to 9 g.
- **Pepitas increased from 3 US tbsp to 70 g, and cashews reduced from ½ US cup (about 70 g) to 40 g**: pepitas have more protein and less sat fat than cashews.
- **Popcorn changed from 2 US cups to 20 g**, about 2½ cups of light Cobs popcorn.
- **"Sprinkle of salt" removed**: the popcorn and fava beans are already salted.
- **Savoury spice made the default.** The paprika and cumin are added to the warm nuts so they stick.

## Notes & swaps
- **Sweet version (from the card):** skip the paprika and cumin, and add 30 g Noshu no-added-sugar choc chips. Adds about 30 kcal and 1 g sat fat per serve (sat fat goes to 4 g, just over the snack target).
- **Choc chickpeas (from the card):** swap 50 g of the fava beans for Happy Snack Co choc-coated chickpeas. Protein drops about 1 g and sugars rise about 3 g per serve.
- **Nut-free:** replace the cashews with extra pepitas or sunflower seeds. Protein rises slightly.
- **Budget:** use plain air-popped popcorn and a store-brand bag of roasted broad beans. No real change to protein.
