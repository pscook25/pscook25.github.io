# Protein Pancakes

Fluffy blended oat, cottage cheese and egg-white pancakes with a hint of banana and cinnamon, stacked with thick Greek yoghurt and tangy raspberries. Over 45 g protein and 14 g fibre in a sweet breakfast that tastes like a weekend treat.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Protein Pancakes" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Batter
- **240 g (2⅔ cups)** rolled oats
- **400 g** light cottage cheese (e.g. Bulla Light)
- **400 g (about 12)** egg whites, or liquid egg whites from a carton
- **200 g (2 medium)** ripe bananas, peeled
- **4 sachets** stevia (e.g. Natvia)
- **2 tsp** ground cinnamon

### To cook
- **8 g (2 tsp)** olive oil, or a light coating of olive oil spray

### To serve
- **600 g** fat-free plain Greek yoghurt (e.g. Chobani 0%)
- **400 g** frozen raspberries, thawed
- **4 sachets** stevia
- **1 tsp** ground cinnamon

## Method
1. Put the oats, cottage cheese, egg whites, bananas, 4 sachets of stevia and 2 tsp cinnamon in a blender or food processor.
2. Blend for 30–60 sec until smooth. Rest the batter for 5 min so the oats thicken it.
3. Heat a large non-stick frying pan over medium heat and brush lightly with a little of the oil.
4. Pour in about 80 mL (⅓ cup) batter per pancake, 2–3 at a time.
5. Cook for about 2 min, until bubbles form on top and the edges look set.
6. Flip and cook 1–2 min more, until golden and firm in the centre.
7. Transfer to a plate and cover with a tea towel to keep warm. Repeat with the remaining oil and batter (about 16 pancakes in total).
8. Stir the remaining 4 sachets of stevia through the Greek yoghurt.
9. Divide the pancakes among 4 plates. Top with the yoghurt and raspberries, and dust with the remaining 1 tsp cinnamon.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 4 pancakes (290 g) plus 150 g yoghurt and 100 g raspberries
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 2 months (pancakes only, layered with baking paper)
- **Reheat:** microwave 4 pancakes for 45–60 sec, or toast from frozen in a toaster for 2 cycles, until hot through (75°C)
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook and chill or freeze the pancakes ahead. Add the yoghurt, raspberries and cinnamon at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Oatmeal is called rolled oats here.
- **Oats increased from ½ US cup (40 g) to 60 g per serve**: adds fibre and energy.
- **Cottage cheese reduced from ½ US cup (about 113 g) to 100 g per serve, and specified as light**: keeps sodium under 700 mg once the yoghurt is added.
- **Added banana to the batter (50 g per serve)**: helps bind the pancakes and adds natural sweetness.
- **Added a Greek yoghurt and raspberry topping (150 g and 100 g per serve)**: the original was about 285 kcal and low in fibre. This brings it to 555 kcal, 48 g protein and 14 g fibre.
- **Stevia split between batter and topping**, since the original used it in both places.

## Notes & swaps
- **Gluten-free:** use certified gluten-free oats (or quinoa flakes). No change to protein.
- **Dairy-free:** replace the cottage cheese with 400 g silken tofu and the Greek yoghurt with a high-protein soy yoghurt. Protein drops to roughly 35 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use frozen mixed berries instead of raspberries. Fibre drops about 2 g per serve.
- **Sweetener:** swap stevia for 2 tsp maple syrup per serve. Adds about 35 kcal and 9 g sugars per serve.
- **Extra protein:** stir 30 g vanilla protein powder into the yoghurt for each serve. Adds about 22 g protein and 115 kcal per serve (still within the energy range only if you skip the banana).
