# Savoury Breakfast Oats

A hearty pan of garlicky turkey mince, mushrooms and peas cooked into thick, creamy oats, with wilted spinach and a tangy splash of balsamic. A savoury, high-protein breakfast bowl that keeps you full until lunch.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Savoury Breakfast Oats" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Turkey & veg
- **10 mL (½ tbsp)** olive oil
- **300 g** button mushrooms, sliced
- **2 cloves (10 g)** garlic, crushed
- **560 g** turkey mince
- **160 g (1 cup)** frozen peas

### Oats
- **240 g (2⅔ cups)** rolled oats
- **320 mL** water
- **50 mL (2½ tbsp)** balsamic vinegar, divided

### To finish
- **240 g (about 4 large handfuls)** baby spinach
- **1 g (pinch)** salt
- **½ tsp** cracked black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan or wide saucepan over medium-high heat.
2. Add the mushrooms and cook for 4–5 min, until golden and any liquid has evaporated.
3. Add the garlic and cook for 30 sec until fragrant.
4. Reduce heat to low-medium, add the turkey mince and cook for 3–4 min, breaking it up, until about half cooked.
5. Drizzle with 30 mL of the balsamic and stir.
6. Add the oats, water and frozen peas and stir well.
7. Cook for 5–6 min, stirring often, until the turkey is cooked through (no pink remaining, internal temp 74°C) and the mixture is thick and holds together.
8. Add the spinach in handfuls and stir for 1–2 min, until wilted.
9. Drizzle with the remaining 20 mL balsamic and remove from the heat.
10. Season with the salt and pepper, then divide among 4 bowls.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 370 g
- **Fridge:** 4 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 2 months
- **Reheat:** microwave covered for 2–2½ min (from frozen, thaw overnight first), stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C). Add 1–2 tbsp water if it has thickened.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the full batch ahead. Add an extra splash of balsamic at serving if you like it tangy.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Ground turkey is called turkey mince here.
- **Turkey reduced from 170 g to 140 g per serve**: protein is still 43 g with the extra oats and veg.
- **Oats increased from ½ US cup (40 g) to 60 g per serve, and water from ¼ US cup to 80 mL per serve**: adds fibre and energy, and the extra water cooks the extra oats.
- **Spinach specified as 60 g per serve** (the card said "1 handful").
- **Added mushrooms, frozen peas, garlic and olive oil**: the original was under the fibre target. These bring it to 11 g fibre and 545 kcal and add flavour.
- **Balsamic quantity specified (12.5 mL per serve)**, since the card said "to taste".
- **Garlic salt (to taste) replaced with fresh garlic, a pinch of salt and pepper**: keeps sodium low at about 255 mg per serve.

## Notes & swaps
- **Vegetarian:** replace the turkey with 400 g canned brown lentils (no added salt, drained) and 200 g crumbled firm tofu. Protein drops to roughly 32 g per serve.
- **Gluten-free:** use certified gluten-free oats or quinoa flakes. No change to protein.
- **Budget:** use 5-star lean beef mince or chicken mince instead of turkey. Protein stays about the same. Beef adds about 1 g sat fat per serve.
- **Extra flavour:** stir through 1 tsp smoked paprika with the garlic, or top with a few drops of hot sauce (adds about 50–90 mg sodium per tsp).
