# Thai Beef Salad

Seared lime-marinated rump steak sliced thin over crisp cos, rice noodles, edamame, bean sprouts, tomato, cucumber and red onion, tossed with mint, coriander and a hot-sour lime dressing and finished with crushed peanuts. Fresh, zingy and packed with 50 g protein.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Thai Beef Salad" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Steak & marinade
- **600 g** lean beef rump or sirloin steak, fat trimmed
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** salt-reduced soy sauce
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** fish sauce
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** lime juice
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** olive oil

### Salad
- **160 g** dried rice vermicelli noodles
- **200 g** frozen shelled edamame
- **400 g (4 hearts)** baby cos lettuce, leaves separated and torn
- **200 g (2 cups)** bean sprouts
- **360 g (3 medium)** tomatoes, cut into wedges
- **400 g (2 Lebanese)** cucumbers, sliced
- **160 g (1 medium)** red onion, thinly sliced
- **60 g (1 bunch mint plus ½ bunch coriander)** mint and coriander leaves

### Dressing & topping
- **80 mL (4 tbsp)** lime juice (about 3 limes)
- **2 tsp** dried chilli flakes
- **40 g** unsalted roasted peanuts, chopped

## Method
1. In a shallow dish, mix the soy sauce, fish sauce and 40 mL lime juice. Add the steak, turn to coat and marinate for 10 min.
2. Meanwhile, put the vermicelli in a heatproof bowl, cover with boiling water and soak for 5–6 min until tender. Drain, rinse under cold water and drain well.
3. Cook the edamame in a small saucepan of boiling water for 3 min. Drain and rinse under cold water.
4. Heat the oil in a large frying pan or chargrill pan over high heat.
5. Remove the steak from the marinade and cook for 2–3 min per side for medium-rare (internal temp 57°C), or to your liking.
6. Transfer to a board and rest for 5 min.
7. In a large bowl, combine the noodles, edamame, cos, bean sprouts, tomato, cucumber, red onion, mint and coriander.
8. Add the 80 mL lime juice and the chilli flakes and toss well.
9. Thinly slice the steak across the grain.
10. Divide the salad among 4 plates, top with the steak and scatter with the peanuts.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 600 g
- **Fridge:** 2 days in airtight containers (steak and dressing stored separately from the leaves) · **Freezer:** not suitable
- **Reheat:** not needed, serve cold or at room temperature
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook and slice the steak, soak the noodles and cook the edamame ahead. Keep the cos, herbs and peanuts separate and add the lime and chilli dressing just before eating so the leaves stay crisp.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric.
- **Steak reduced from 170 g to 150 g per serve, specified as trimmed rump or sirloin**: protein is still 50 g.
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos replaced with salt-reduced soy sauce (5 mL per serve)**: easier to find at Coles, Woolworths and Aldi.
- **Added fish sauce to the ingredients list (2.5 mL per serve)**: it was in the method but not listed.
- **Lime juice increased** so there's enough for both the marinade and the dressing, as the method describes.
- **Added olive oil** for cooking the steak.
- **Chilli flakes reduced from 1 tsp per serve to 2 tsp in total.**
- **Added rice vermicelli, edamame and peanuts**: the original was about 385 kcal with about 6 g fibre. These bring it to 565 kcal and 10 g fibre and make it a full meal.
- **Vegetable quantities given in grams** (e.g. 50 g bean sprouts, 100 g cucumber per serve). The red onion is reduced from ½ to ¼ onion per serve.

## Notes & swaps
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos:** use 15 mL in place of the soy sauce if you prefer it (health food aisle at Woolworths). Sodium stays about the same.
- **Gluten-free:** use salt-reduced tamari instead of soy sauce and check the fish sauce label.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the steak with 600 g firm tofu, pressed, sliced and pan-fried until golden, and use soy sauce instead of fish sauce. Protein drops to roughly 35 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use beef stir-fry strips, cooked quickly for 1–2 min. Protein stays about the same.
- **Nut-free:** replace the peanuts with 40 g toasted sesame seeds. Protein drops about 1 g per serve.
