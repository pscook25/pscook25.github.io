# Avocado Tuna Salad

Creamy mashed avocado folded through flaked tuna, juicy tomato, kidney beans, red onion and coriander with lime and a chilli kick, served over brown rice and salad leaves. A no-cook lunch with 44 g protein and 14 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Avocado Tuna Salad" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Tuna mix
- **240 g (about 1½ large)** avocados, flesh only
- **4 × 185 g cans** tuna in springwater, drained (about 480 g drained)
- **600 g (4 medium)** tomatoes, diced
- **400 g can** no-added-salt red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- **80 g (½ small)** red onion, finely diced
- **20 g (½ bunch)** coriander, leaves chopped

### Dressing
- **90 mL (4½ tbsp)** lime juice (about 3 limes)
- **2 g (1 tsp)** chilli powder
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** sweet chilli or hot chilli sauce, to serve

### To serve
- **400 g** cooked brown rice (2 × 250 g microwave pouches, or from 135 g / ⅔ cup uncooked)
- **160 g** mixed salad leaves

## Method
1. Heat the brown rice according to the packet (or cook it ahead and cool). Spread on a plate to cool for 5 min.
2. In a large bowl, mash the avocado with a fork until mostly smooth.
3. Add the tuna and tomato and mash gently to combine, leaving some texture.
4. Stir in the kidney beans, red onion and coriander.
5. Stir in the lime juice and chilli powder.
6. Divide the salad leaves and rice among 4 bowls.
7. Top with the tuna mixture and drizzle with chilli sauce.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 500 g
- **Fridge:** 1 day in airtight containers (press plastic wrap onto the surface to slow browning) · **Freezer:** not suitable
- **Reheat:** not needed, serve cold
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the rice and mix the tuna, tomato, beans, onion and coriander up to 2 days ahead. Mash in the avocado and lime on the day so it stays green. Add chilli sauce at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric.
- **Tuna specified as 120 g drained per serve** (the card's 1½ cans) in springwater, using Australian 185 g cans.
- **Avocado reduced from ½ to about ⅓ avocado per serve (60 g)**: keeps sat fat at 4 g.
- **Tomato changed from 1 US cup to 150 g per serve.**
- **Lime juice kept at 1½ US tbsp per serve (90 mL in total).**
- **Chilli powder reduced from ½ tsp per serve to 1 tsp in total**: Australian chilli powder is much hotter than a US chili powder blend.
- **Chilli sauce given a quantity (5 mL per serve).**
- **Added kidney beans, red onion, coriander, brown rice and salad leaves**: the original was about 385 kcal with 5 g fibre. These bring it to 555 kcal and 14 g fibre and make it a full lunch.

## Notes & swaps
- **No-added-salt tuna:** use John West or Sirena no-added-salt tuna to cut sodium by about 300 mg per serve. No change to protein.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the tuna with 2 × 400 g cans no-added-salt chickpeas, roughly mashed. Protein drops to roughly 20 g per serve, below target. Add 200 g crumbled firm tofu to reach about 30 g.
- **Lower carb:** skip the rice and add a second can of kidney beans. Energy drops to about 500 kcal and fibre rises to about 19 g.
- **Budget:** use a 425 g can of tuna plus one 185 g can instead of four small cans.
