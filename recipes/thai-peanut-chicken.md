# Thai Peanut Chicken

Tender poached chicken and crisp-tender veg tossed in a creamy, zesty peanut sauce with a cayenne kick, served over brown rice with edamame, coriander and lime. A satisfying bowl with 55 g protein and 12 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Thai Peanut Chicken" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- **600 g** skinless chicken breast fillets
- **1 L** water, for poaching

### Peanut sauce
- **100 g (5 tbsp)** natural peanut butter, no added salt (e.g. Mayver's or Bega Simply Nuts)
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** salt-reduced soy sauce
- **60 mL (3 tbsp)** lemon juice (about 1½ lemons)
- **60 mL (3 tbsp)** rice vinegar
- **1 tsp** cayenne pepper (use ½ tsp for mild)
- **60–80 mL** hot water, to thin

### Veg & base
- **200 g** broccoli, cut into small florets
- **200 g (1 medium)** red capsicum, sliced
- **200 g (2 small)** carrots, julienned or grated
- **200 g** snow peas, trimmed
- **200 g** frozen shelled edamame
- **400 g** cooked brown rice (from 140 g / ⅔ cup uncooked, or microwave brown rice pouches)

### To serve
- **2** spring onions, thinly sliced
- **15 g (½ bunch)** coriander, leaves picked
- **1** lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. If cooking rice from scratch, cook the brown rice according to the packet, about 25–30 min. (Microwave pouches can be heated at step 10.)
2. Place the chicken in a medium saucepan and cover with the 1 L water.
3. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, then reduce to low, cover and poach for 12–15 min, until the internal temp reaches 75°C.
4. Lift the chicken onto a board and rest for 5 min.
5. Meanwhile, microwave the peanut butter in a large heatproof bowl for 15–20 sec until softened.
6. Add the soy sauce, lemon juice, rice vinegar and cayenne and whisk until thick and smooth.
7. Whisk in the hot water a little at a time until the sauce is a coating consistency.
8. Bring a separate saucepan of water to the boil. Add the broccoli, carrot and edamame and blanch for 2 min.
9. Add the snow peas and capsicum and blanch 1 min more, until bright and tender-crisp. Drain well.
10. Heat the cooked brown rice (or microwave pouches) until steaming.
11. Shred the chicken with two forks.
12. Add the shredded chicken and drained veg to the peanut sauce and toss until coated.
13. Taste and add a squeeze of lime rather than salt.
14. Divide the rice among 4 bowls, top with the peanut chicken and scatter with spring onion and coriander. Serve with lime wedges.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 510 g
- **Fridge:** 4 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** 2 months (chicken and sauce only; the veg goes soft, and rice freezes separately)
- **Reheat:** microwave covered for 2–2½ min, stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C). Add 1 tbsp water if the sauce has thickened. Also good cold as a lunch bowl.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the chicken, sauce, rice and blanched veg ahead. Add the spring onion, coriander and lime wedges at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric (20 mL tbsp).
- **Chicken reduced from 170 g to 150 g per serve**: protein is still well above target (55 g), and it keeps energy within 650 kcal once rice is added.
- **Peanut butter reduced from 2 US tbsp (about 32 g) to 25 g per serve, and switched to natural no-added-salt**: controls fat, sat fat and sodium.
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos (2 tbsp per serve) replaced with salt-reduced soy sauce (10 mL per serve)**: the original amount alone would put each serve well over 700 mg sodium, and salt-reduced soy is easier to find at Coles, Woolworths and Aldi.
- **"Top with salt to taste" replaced with lime**: keeps sodium at about 475 mg per serve.
- **Vegetables increased from 1 US cup to 200 g per serve, and specified**: lifts fibre and volume.
- **Added edamame (50 g per serve)**: boosts fibre and plant protein.
- **Added brown rice (100 g cooked per serve)**: the original was about 410 kcal and 5 g fibre. This brings energy into the 500–650 kcal range and fibre to 12 g.
- **Added a poaching step**: the card assumed the chicken was already cooked.
- **Added spring onion, coriander and lime** to serve, for freshness.

## Notes & swaps
- **Bragg's Liquid Aminos:** if you prefer it, use 30 mL (1½ tbsp) in total. Sodium stays about the same. It's in the health food aisle at Woolworths or health food stores.
- **Gluten-free:** use salt-reduced tamari instead of soy sauce. No change to protein.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the chicken with 600 g firm tofu (pressed, cubed and pan-fried) plus a second 200 g bag of edamame. Protein drops to roughly 35 g per serve, still on target.
- **Shortcut:** use 480 g shredded BBQ chicken breast meat (skin removed). Protein is similar, but sodium rises by about 200–300 mg per serve, so cut the soy to 30 mL.
- **Budget:** use a 1 kg bag of frozen stir-fry veg instead of fresh, and trimmed chicken thigh fillets. Protein drops about 5 g and sat fat rises about 1 g per serve, still within targets.
- **Nut allergy:** use tahini instead of peanut butter, if sesame is tolerated. Protein is similar and fibre slightly higher.
