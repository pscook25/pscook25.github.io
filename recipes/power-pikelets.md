# Power Pikelets

Fluffy little banana pikelets with vanilla protein and a few melty choc chips, cooked golden in minutes and topped with tangy raspberries. A quick pre-workout snack with 21 g protein.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Power Pikelets" (author unknown; serves 3)

## Nutrition (per serve, estimated)
| Energy | Protein | Carbs | Sugars | Fat | Sat fat | Fibre | Sodium |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 260 kcal / 1100 kJ | 21 g | 27 g | 14 g | 7 g | 3 g | 7 g | 220 mg |

*A serve is about 6 pikelets with raspberries. Estimates based on AUSNUT 2011–13 / FSANZ data and typical labels (whey protein powder, 30 g per scoop). Snack targets apply: 200–300 kcal, ≥15 g protein, ≥4 g fibre, sat fat ≤3 g, sodium ≤350 mg.*

## Ingredients

### Batter
- **120 g (1 medium)** ripe banana, peeled
- **50 g (1)** egg
- **60 g (2)** egg whites
- **30 g (3 US tbsp, about 2 Aus tbsp)** wholemeal self-raising flour
- **30 g (1 scoop)** vanilla or chocolate whey protein powder
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** unsweetened almond milk
- **10 g (1 tbsp)** Noshu no-added-sugar dark choc chips

### To cook & serve
- **2 g** olive oil spray
- **100 g** frozen raspberries, thawed

## Method
1. Mash the banana in a bowl until smooth.
2. Add the egg, egg whites, flour, protein powder and almond milk and mix until just combined.
3. Stir in the choc chips.
4. Heat a large non-stick frying pan over low heat and spray lightly with oil.
5. Add 6 spoonfuls of batter (about 1 heaped tbsp each) to the pan.
6. Cook for 1–2 min until bubbles appear and the base is lightly browned.
7. Flip and cook the other side for 1–2 min, until golden and cooked through.
8. Repeat with the remaining batter (about 12 pikelets in total).
9. Divide between 2 plates and top with the raspberries.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 6 pikelets (130 g) plus 50 g raspberries
- **Fridge:** 3 days in an airtight container · **Freezer:** 2 months (layer with baking paper)
- **Reheat:** microwave 6 pikelets for 20–30 sec, or toast from frozen in a toaster, until warm
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** best fresh, but they freeze well. Add the raspberries at serving.

## Changes from original
- **Serves changed from 3 to 2, and protein powder (optional on the card) increased to 1 scoop**: lifts protein from about 4 g to 21 g per serve and energy into the snack range.
- **Added 2 egg whites**: more protein without adding sat fat.
- **Self-raising flour changed to wholemeal self-raising, and raspberries added to serve**: lifts fibre to 7 g per serve.
- **Almond milk (2 tsp per the card's protein note) set at 20 mL**, to suit the extra protein powder and egg whites.
- **Optional stevia left out**: the banana and protein powder make them sweet enough.

## Notes & swaps
- **No choc chips (from the card):** swap the choc chips for ½ grated apple or pear. Sat fat drops to about 2 g per serve.
- **Dairy-free:** use a pea or soy protein powder. Protein drops about 1 g per serve.
- **Gluten-free:** use a gluten-free self-raising flour. Fibre drops about 1 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use frozen mixed berries instead of raspberries. Fibre drops about 1 g per serve.
