# Garlic Prawns

Juicy prawns tossed in plenty of garlic, tomato, red wine vinegar and basil, pan-cooked with cannellini beans and baby spinach and spooned over fluffy white rice. Big garlic flavour with 45 g protein and 11 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Garlic Shrimp" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Prawns & marinade
- **600 g** raw peeled prawns, deveined, tails removed (about 1.2 kg whole raw prawns, or frozen raw peeled)
- **40 g (about 8 cloves, or 2 tbsp jarred)** garlic, minced
- **600 g (4 medium)** tomatoes, cut into 1 cm cubes
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** red wine vinegar
- **2 tsp** dried basil
- **3 g (½ tsp)** salt
- **¼ tsp** cracked black pepper

### Pan
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** olive oil
- **400 g can** no-added-salt cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- **120 g (4 cups loosely packed)** baby spinach

### To serve
- **600 g** cooked white rice (from 200 g / 1 cup uncooked jasmine rice, or microwave rice pouches)

## Method
1. Cook the rice according to the packet, about 15 min. Keep warm.
2. In a large bowl, combine the garlic, tomato, vinegar, basil, salt and pepper.
3. Add the prawns and toss until thoroughly coated.
4. Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan over medium heat.
5. Pour in the prawn mixture and cook, turning the prawns once, for 3–4 min, until pink and opaque all the way through (internal temp 63°C).
6. Add the cannellini beans and spinach and stir for 1–2 min, until the beans are hot and the spinach has wilted.
7. Remove from the heat. Divide the rice among 4 bowls and spoon the prawns and sauce over the top.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 480 g (150 g rice plus the prawn mixture)
- **Fridge:** 2 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** not suitable (the prawns go rubbery)
- **Reheat:** microwave covered for 1½–2 min, stirring halfway, until piping hot (75°C). Don't overheat or the prawns toughen.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** cook the rice and chop the tomato and garlic ahead. The prawns take 5 min, so they're best cooked fresh.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 1 serve to 4**, converted to Australian metric. Shrimp is called prawns here.
- **Prawns reduced from 170 g to 150 g per serve**: protein is still 45 g with the beans added.
- **Salt cut from ½ tsp per serve to ½ tsp in total**: the original amount alone was about 1,150 mg sodium per serve.
- **Garlic reduced from 2 US tbsp per serve to 40 g in total (10 g per serve)**: still very garlicky, but not overpowering at 4 serves.
- **Tomato changed from 1 US cup to 150 g per serve.**
- **Red wine vinegar and dried basil given quantities**, since the card gave none.
- **Added olive oil** for the pan, since the card didn't say how to stop the prawns sticking.
- **Added cannellini beans and baby spinach**: the original had about 3 g fibre. These bring it to 11 g and make it a fuller meal.
- **Rice set at 150 g cooked per serve** (the card's ½ cup, read as cooked rice based on its nutrition panel).

## Notes & swaps
- **Brown rice:** use cooked brown rice instead of white. Fibre rises about 2 g per serve. No change to protein.
- **Pre-cooked prawns:** use 480 g cooked peeled prawns and just warm through in step 5. Sodium rises by 200–400 mg per serve depending on brand, so leave out the salt.
- **Vegetarian:** replace the prawns with 2 × 400 g cans no-added-salt chickpeas plus 300 g firm tofu, cubed. Protein drops to roughly 30 g per serve.
- **Budget:** use frozen raw peeled prawns (Aldi and Coles both stock them), or swap to 600 g diced chicken breast, cooked for 6–7 min until internal temp reaches 75°C. Protein stays about the same.
- **Fresh basil:** use a small handful of torn fresh basil at the end instead of the dried basil.
