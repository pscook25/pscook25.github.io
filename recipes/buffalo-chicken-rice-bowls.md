# Buffalo Chicken Rice Bowls

Tangy, spicy shredded buffalo chicken over a brown rice and cauliflower rice base, piled with chickpeas, crunchy salad, creamy avocado and a cool yoghurt ranch drizzle. Lean poached breast and a homemade ranch keep it big on protein and fibre while staying low in saturated fat and sodium.

**Source:** Fika Nutrition

## Ingredients

### Buffalo chicken
- **650 g** chicken breast fillets, trimmed (yields ~500 g cooked)
- **1.5 L** cold water, for poaching
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** cayenne pepper hot sauce (e.g. Frank's RedHot Original), divided
- **1 tsp** smoked paprika

### Base
- **140 g (about ¾ cup)** brown rice, rinsed
- **400 mL** water, for the rice
- **400 g** frozen cauliflower rice (e.g. Birds Eye)
- **1 × 400 g can** no-added-salt chickpeas, drained and rinsed (~240 g drained)

### Yoghurt ranch
- **150 g** reduced-fat Greek-style yoghurt
- **20 mL (1 tbsp)** lemon juice, divided
- **1 tsp** dried dill
- **½ tsp** garlic powder
- **½ tsp** onion powder
- **Pinch** freshly ground black pepper
- **5–10 mL (1–2 tsp)** water, to thin

### Toppings
- **160 g (about 4 cups)** cos or iceberg lettuce, shredded
- **300 g (2 medium)** capsicums, mixed colours, diced
- **300 g** cherry tomatoes, halved
- **240 g (2)** Lebanese cucumbers, diced
- **40 g (¼ cup)** red onion, finely chopped
- **25 g (¼ cup, about 2)** spring onions, thinly sliced
- **1 medium** avocado (~170 g flesh)

## Method
1. Place the rinsed brown rice and 400 mL water in a small saucepan and bring to the boil over high heat.
2. Cover, reduce heat to low and simmer for 25–30 min, until the water is absorbed and the grains are tender.
3. Take the rice off the heat and stand, covered, for 5 min, then fluff with a fork and spread on a tray to cool.
4. Meanwhile, place the chicken in a medium saucepan and cover with 1.5 L cold water.
5. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium–high heat, then reduce to low and poach for 12–15 min, until the internal temp reaches 75°C.
6. Lift the chicken onto a board, rest for 5 min, then shred with two forks.
7. Cook the cauliflower rice according to the packet (about 4–5 min in the microwave) until hot, drain off any liquid and spread out to cool.
8. In a small bowl, whisk the yoghurt, 2 tsp lemon juice, dill, garlic powder, onion powder and pepper.
9. Add water 1 tsp at a time until the ranch is thin enough to drizzle.
10. In a medium bowl, mix 30 mL (1½ tbsp) of the hot sauce with the smoked paprika, reserving the remaining 10 mL (2 tsp) for drizzling.
11. Add the shredded chicken and toss until evenly coated. Start with less sauce and add more a little at a time to reach your preferred heat.
12. Prepare the lettuce, capsicum, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion and spring onion as listed.
13. Halve, stone and slice the avocado, then toss gently with the remaining 2 tsp lemon juice to slow browning.
14. Divide the brown rice and cauliflower rice between 4 bowls or containers (about 100 g of each per bowl).
15. Top each with a quarter of the buffalo chicken (about 125 g) and a quarter of the chickpeas (about 60 g).
16. Arrange the lettuce, capsicum, tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, spring onion and avocado over the top.
17. Drizzle each bowl with the yoghurt ranch and the reserved hot sauce.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** approx. 730 g per serve
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** not suitable (fresh salad, avocado and yoghurt don't freeze well)
- **Reheat:** If you prefer it warm, move the salad aside (or pack it separately), then microwave the rice, chicken and chickpeas on high for 1½–2 min, stirring halfway, until steaming hot (75°C). Add the cold salad and ranch after reheating.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** Cook the rice, cauli rice, chicken and ranch ahead; cool the rice quickly and refrigerate within an hour. Keep the ranch in small separate pots. Add the avocado on the day, or slice it fresh each morning for the best colour; pack the tomatoes and cucumber separately if you like a crisper salad on day 3.

## Changes from original
- **Cooked chicken (500 g) → 650 g raw chicken breast, poached at home:** gives the same ~500 g cooked, but skinless and unseasoned, avoiding the extra sodium and saturated fat of BBQ/rotisserie chicken.
- **Hot sauce reduced from ½ cup (125 mL) to 2 tbsp (40 mL):** ½ cup of a typical cayenne hot sauce alone adds roughly 1,200 mg sodium per serve, well over the 700 mg target.
- **Smoked paprika added:** adds depth and colour to the smaller amount of hot sauce without any sodium.
- **Bottled ranch (¼ cup) → homemade yoghurt ranch:** cuts saturated fat and sodium and adds about 3 g protein per serve.
- **White rice → brown rice, plus 400 g cauliflower rice (the original's optional suggestion, now included):** raises fibre and bulks the bowls without many extra kilojoules.
- **Chickpeas increased from 1 cup to a full 400 g can, no-added-salt:** lifts fibre and protein; the no-added-salt version saves about 150 mg sodium per serve.
- **"Unlimited" salad vegetables given set quantities:** needed to estimate nutrition. Feel free to add more; extra salad adds very little energy.
- **Lemon juice added to the avocado:** slows browning for meal prep.
- **Energy:** the original states 487 kcal per serve; these changes bring it to ~590 kcal, inside the 500–650 kcal range.
- **Wording:** green onion → spring onion (Australian name).

## Notes & swaps
- **Vegetarian:** Replace the chicken with 600 g firm tofu, crumbled, tossed in the buffalo sauce and baked at 200°C conventional / 180°C fan-forced for 20–25 min until golden. Protein drops to about 36 g per serve, still above target.
- **Dairy-free:** Make the ranch with plain unsweetened soy yoghurt (protein drops by about 1–2 g per serve). Avoid coconut yoghurt, which would push saturated fat up by roughly 3–4 g per serve.
- **Budget:** Use trimmed chicken thigh fillets instead of breast (protein drops about 4 g, sat fat rises about 1.5 g, still within target). Iceberg lettuce and Aldi's own-brand frozen cauli rice and canned chickpeas also work well; protein is unchanged.
- **Lower carb:** Swap all the brown rice for cauliflower rice. Energy drops to about 465 kcal, below target, so add an extra half-can of chickpeas or another ¼ avocado per serve to bring it back up. Protein stays about the same.
- **Hot sauce:** Any cayenne-style hot sauce works, but check the label and aim for one under about 4,000 mg sodium per 100 mL. For extra heat without salt, add a pinch of chilli flakes instead of more sauce.
