# Honey Mustard Chicken Salad

Warm, smoky-spiced chicken and caramelised sweet potato over crisp spinach, tomato, red onion and chickpeas, tossed in a tangy wholegrain honey mustard dressing. Lean chicken and chickpeas carry the protein and fibre, while a much lighter dressing keeps the energy in range.

**Source:** Unknown (recipe card: "Honey Mustard Chicken Salad", serves 2)

## Ingredients

### Roast sweet potato
- **600 g** orange sweet potato (kumara), unpeeled, cut into 2 cm cubes
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** extra virgin olive oil

### Chicken
- **600 g** skinless chicken breast fillets (about 3)
- **10 mL (2 tsp)** extra virgin olive oil
- **1 tsp** smoked paprika
- **1 tsp** garlic powder
- **¼ tsp** salt
- **½ tsp** cracked black pepper

### Honey mustard dressing
- **40 g (2 tbsp)** wholegrain mustard
- **20 g (1 tbsp)** Dijon mustard
- **40 mL (2 tbsp)** extra virgin olive oil
- **28 g (1 tbsp)** honey
- **30 mL (1½ tbsp)** lemon juice (about 1 lemon)

### Salad
- **240 g** baby spinach (2 × 120 g bags)
- **480 g** ripe tomatoes (4 medium), cut into wedges
- **100 g (⅔ cup)** red onion (1 small), thinly sliced
- **400 g** can no-added-salt chickpeas, drained and rinsed (240 g drained)

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 220°C conventional (200°C fan-forced). Line a large baking tray with baking paper.
2. Toss the sweet potato with 10 mL oil on the tray and spread out in a single layer.
3. Roast for 25–30 min, turning halfway, until golden at the edges and tender when pierced.
4. While the sweet potato roasts, place each chicken breast between baking paper and pound to an even 2 cm thickness.
5. Mix the paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Rub the chicken with 10 mL oil, then the spice mix.
6. Heat a large non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5–6 min each side, until golden and the internal temperature reaches 75°C.
7. Rest the chicken on a board for 5 min, then slice thinly.
8. Whisk the wholegrain mustard, Dijon, 40 mL oil, honey and lemon juice in a small bowl until thick and fully combined.
9. Place the spinach, tomatoes, red onion and chickpeas in a large bowl.
10. Add the warm sweet potato and toss gently.
11. Top with the warm sliced chicken, drizzle with the dressing and serve straight away.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** approx. 530 g per serve
- **Fridge:** 3 days in airtight containers · **Freezer:** not suitable (the assembled salad); cooked chicken alone freezes for up to 2 months
- **Reheat:** Reheat only the chicken and sweet potato, in the microwave on high for 1–1½ min until steaming hot (75°C), then add to the cold salad.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** Cook the chicken and sweet potato and make the dressing up to 3 days ahead. Pack the dressing in a small separate pot. Keep the spinach, tomato, onion and chickpeas in their own container so the spinach stays crisp. Dress just before eating.

## Changes from original
- **Scaled from 2 to 4 serves.** Converted cups/tablespoons to Australian metric with gram weights.
- **"1 bag spinach" given a weight:** the card doesn't state a bag size, so I used 120 g per original 2 serves (a standard Coles/Woolworths bag), i.e. 240 g for 4.
- **Chicken increased from 85 g to 150 g raw per serve** so protein reaches 46 g. The card's 37 g protein isn't achievable from 85 g chicken.
- **Olive oil cut from 8 tbsp (for 2) to 3 tbsp total (for 4).** The original works out to about 4 tbsp oil (~65 g fat, ~600 kcal) per serve from oil alone. That doesn't match the card's 365 kcal / 20 g fat, so the card's nutrition panel is unreliable. The new amount keeps the dressing glossy but brings fat and saturated fat into range.
- **Stevia replaced with 1 tbsp honey.** It makes it a true honey mustard. Adds ~6 g sugars per serve; swap back to stevia if you prefer (see notes).
- **Mustard reduced from ⅓ cup (for 2) to 60 g total (for 4), split wholegrain + Dijon**, and lemon juice added. That keeps the tang, adds texture, and helps keep sodium under 700 mg.
- **Added roast sweet potato and chickpeas** to lift energy into the 500–650 kcal range and fibre from ~4 g to 12 g per serve.
- **Added a simple spice rub and an internal-temperature cue** for the chicken. The original doesn't say how to cook it.
- Kept the original serving style: cold salad with warm chicken.

## Notes & swaps
- **Vegetarian:** Replace the chicken with 2 × 300 g blocks of firm tofu, pressed, cubed and pan-fried with the same rub, and add a second can of chickpeas. Protein drops to roughly 32–35 g per serve.
- **Dairy-free:** Already dairy-free as written.
- **Budget:** Use chicken thigh fillets, trimmed (adds ~1–2 g sat fat per serve, still under target), and supermarket-brand mustards. Protein stays about the same.
- **Lower sugar:** Use 2 sachets of stevia (as in the original) instead of honey. This cuts ~6 g sugars and 20 kcal per serve. Protein is unchanged.
- **Swap the sweet potato** for 150 g (dry weight) brown rice or quinoa cooked to packet directions if you'd rather a grain. Protein goes up 1–2 g per serve with quinoa.
- **Spinach:** Rocket or a mixed salad leaf bag works equally well.
