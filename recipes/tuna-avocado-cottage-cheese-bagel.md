# Tuna, Avocado & Cottage Cheese Bagel

A toasted wholemeal bagel spread with cool cottage cheese, piled with peppery tuna and red onion, and topped with creamy lemony smashed avocado. Hearty, fresh and filling, with plenty of protein and fibre to keep you going through the afternoon.

**Source:** Original

## Ingredients
### Bagel
- **1 (about 90 g)** wholemeal bagel, halved

### Tuna
- **1 × 185 g can (about 130 g drained)** tuna in springwater, no added salt, well drained
- **20 g** red onion, very thinly sliced
- **1 tsp (5 mL)** lemon juice
- **1/4 tsp** freshly cracked black pepper

### To top
- **40 g (about 2 tbsp)** cottage cheese
- **110 g (about 3/4 medium avocado)** ripe avocado
- **2 tsp (10 mL)** lemon juice
- **1 small pinch (about 0.1 g)** salt

## Method
1. Drain the tuna very well, pressing out the water.
2. Flake the tuna into a small bowl.
3. Add the red onion, 1 tsp lemon juice and the pepper, then mix gently.
4. Halve the bagel and toast the cut sides until golden, 2–3 min.
5. Scoop the avocado flesh into a bowl.
6. Add the 2 tsp lemon juice and the pinch of salt, then roughly smash with a fork.
7. Spread the cottage cheese over both toasted bagel halves.
8. Divide the tuna mixture between the halves.
9. Top with the smashed avocado and serve straight away.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** approx. 390 g (1 loaded bagel)
- **Fridge:** 3 days (tuna mix only, airtight container) · **Freezer:** not suitable
- **Reheat:** not needed. Serve the tuna mix cold from the fridge (keep at 5°C or below) and toast the bagel fresh.
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** mix the tuna, onion, lemon and pepper up to 3 days ahead. Toast the bagel, smash the avocado and assemble just before eating so nothing goes soggy or brown. Wholemeal bagels can be frozen sliced for up to 2 months and toasted from frozen.

## Changes from original
- No quantities given, so a single serve was built from the listed ingredients (tuna, avocado, cottage cheese, bagel, red onion, salt, pepper).
- Specified a wholemeal bagel to lift fibre to 13 g per serve (target ≥8 g).
- Specified tuna in springwater with no added salt to keep sodium under 700 mg.
- Limited salt to a tiny pinch on the avocado only, as the bagel, tuna and cottage cheese already contribute sodium.
- Added lemon juice (not in the original list) to season without extra salt and to stop the avocado browning.
- Set the tuna amount at a full 185 g can and the avocado at 3/4 medium to reach ≥30 g protein and 500–650 kcal.

## Notes & swaps
- **Sodium:** this is the tightest target. Use a lower-salt bagel and no-added-salt tuna where you can. If your bagel is over about 450 mg sodium per 90 g, drop the salt entirely.
- **Higher protein:** swap the cottage cheese for 60 g (but check its sodium) or add a boiled egg (+6 g protein).
- **Lower protein, budget:** use a 95 g can of tuna (about 70 g drained) instead, which drops protein by roughly 15 g to about 34 g and lowers kcal and sodium.
- **Dairy-free:** replace the cottage cheese with extra smashed avocado or a thin spread of hummus (about −4 g protein).
- **Vegetarian:** replace the tuna with a mashed 100 g of canned, rinsed chickpeas plus a boiled egg (protein drops to about 30 g).
