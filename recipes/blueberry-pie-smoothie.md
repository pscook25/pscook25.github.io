# Blueberry Pie Smoothie

A thick, purple berry smoothie with vanilla, cinnamon and a spoonful of oats for that pie-crust flavour. The spinach hides completely. A quick shake with 28 g protein and 8 g fibre.

**Source:** Recipe card, "Shake Recipes: Blueberry Pie Smoothie" (author unknown; serves 1)

## Ingredients

### Smoothie
- **30 g (1 scoop)** vanilla whey protein powder
- **95 g (⅔ US cup)** frozen mixed berries
- **60 g (2 large handfuls)** baby spinach
- **20 g (¼ cup)** rolled oats
- **250 mL (1 cup)** cold water
- **5 mL (1 tsp)** vanilla extract
- **Pinch** ground cinnamon
- **2 sachets** stevia (e.g. Natvia), or to taste

## Method
1. Put the water, spinach and oats in a blender and blend for 30 sec until the spinach is smooth.
2. Add the berries, protein powder, vanilla, cinnamon and stevia.
3. Blend for 30–60 sec until thick and smooth.
4. Pour into a glass and serve straight away.

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Portion:** about 460 mL
- **Fridge:** 1 day in a sealed bottle (shake before drinking) · **Freezer:** not suitable once blended
- **Reheat:** not needed
- **Cook ahead vs fresh:** pack the berries, spinach and oats into freezer bags for up to 3 months, then tip into the blender with the water and powder.

## Changes from original
- **Added rolled oats (20 g)**: lifts energy into the snack range, adds fibre, and gives the "pie crust" flavour.
- **Quantities written in grams and Australian metric.**

## Notes & swaps
- **Blueberries:** use frozen blueberries instead of mixed berries for a stronger blueberry flavour. Fibre drops about 1 g.
- **Creamier:** use 250 mL skim milk instead of water. Adds about 90 kcal and 9 g protein (just over the snack energy range).
- **Dairy-free:** use a vanilla pea or soy protein powder. Protein drops about 1 g.
- **Gluten-free:** use certified gluten-free oats or quinoa flakes.
